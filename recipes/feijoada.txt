Feijoada
portions 12

pantry:
1 kg dry black beans
1 cube picanha bouillon
8 g MSG
45 g tomato paste
20 g white miso
produce:
3 large onions, white or brown, finely chopped
38 g garlic, minced
dairy:
60 g unsalted butter
meat:
1.5 kg pork butt, or shoulder, cut in 2.5 cm cubes
1.5 kg linguiça, or Portuguese sausage, cut in 1 cm coins
450 g thick-cut bacon, cut in 1 to 2 cm pieces
spices:
1.5 g cayenne
1.7 g black pepper
9 g curry powder
kitchen:
3 l water

dry black beans > sort: Sort out stones and bad beans and rinse well; do not soak.
unsalted butter + large onions + garlic + tomato paste + curry powder + cayenne + black pepper > onion paste: Melt the butter on sauté and cook everything, stirring often, until soft, dark and paste-like. ~30 min [pressure cooker]
pork butt > roast pork: Spread on parchment-lined trays in a single layer and roast. ~30 min [oven 450 F fan]
linguiça > roast sausage: Spread the coins on lined trays and roast at the same heat. ~18 min [oven 450 F fan]
thick-cut bacon > roast bacon: Spread on a lined tray and roast at 425 F fan; keep the rendered fat. ~18 min [oven 425 F fan]
onion paste + sort + water + picanha bouillon + MSG + roast pork + roast sausage + roast bacon > pressure: Add the beans, water, bouillon, MSG and all the meats with the bacon fat to the pressure cooker. Cook on high pressure 85 minutes, then natural release 30 minutes. ~1 h 55 min
pressure > thicken: Skim excess fat and mash beans against the side of the pot until the sauce thickens.
thicken + white miso > finish: Off the heat, stir in the miso and rest 10 minutes before serving. ~10 min
