Rich red wine beef stew (Instant Pot)
portions 12
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
2 kg boneless beef shoulder roast, butcher-trimmed (arm roast or cross-rib)
produce:
1.1 kg Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into 3 cm chunks
550 g carrots, cut into 2 cm pieces
300 g yellow onion, diced
20 g garlic, minced
pantry:
35 g tomato paste
225 ml dry red wine
1 l low-sodium beef stock
25 ml Worcestershire sauce
45 g cornstarch
35 ml neutral oil
spices:
2 g MSG
2 g dried thyme
12 g salt, plus more to taste
2 g black pepper
kitchen:
90 ml cold water
optional:
120 g celery, diced
15 g fresh parsley, chopped
5 ml red wine vinegar

boneless beef shoulder roast + salt + black pepper > prep beef: Remove only large exterior fat lumps; leave marbling and small seams. Cut into 4–5 cm cubes, pat dry and season. ~10 min
prep beef + neutral oil > brown: Sauté (High): brown about one-third of the beef in 2 batches; set aside with the remaining uncooked beef. ~15 min [8-quart Instant Pot]
yellow onion + celery + garlic + tomato paste > aromatics: Sauté (Normal): cook onion and optional celery 5–7 minutes. Add garlic and tomato paste; cook 1 minute. ~8 min
aromatics + dry red wine > deglaze: Pour in wine, scrape the bottom completely clean and simmer until reduced by half. Press Cancel. ~5 min
brown + deglaze + low-sodium beef stock + Worcestershire sauce + MSG + dried thyme > pressure: Add all the beef and juices, stock and seasonings. Seal and cook on High Pressure for 45 minutes. Allow full natural pressure release. If still chewy, cook another 8–10 minutes under pressure and let release naturally. ~1 h 25 min
pressure + Yukon Gold potatoes + carrots > vegetables: Add potatoes and carrots. On Sauté (Normal), simmer uncovered for 25–40 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender. Add a splash of water if needed. ~35 min
cornstarch + cold water > slurry: Whisk until smooth. [bowl]
vegetables + slurry + fresh parsley + red wine vinegar > finish: Stir in slurry gradually while simmering; cook 1–2 minutes until gravy is thick. Adjust salt; add optional parsley and vinegar to taste. ~3 min
