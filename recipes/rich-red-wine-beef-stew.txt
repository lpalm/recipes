Rich red wine beef stew (Instant Pot)
portions 12
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
2 kg boneless chuck-eye roast
produce:
1.1 kg Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into 3 cm chunks
550 g carrots, cut into 2 cm pieces
300 g yellow onion, diced
20 g garlic, minced
pantry:
35 g tomato paste
225 ml dry red wine
1 l low-sodium beef stock
25 ml Worcestershire sauce
45 g cornstarch
35 ml neutral oil
spices:
2 g MSG
2 g dried thyme
12 g salt, plus more to taste
2 g black pepper
kitchen:
90 ml cold water
optional:
120 g celery, diced
1 bay leaf
15 g fresh parsley, chopped
5 ml red wine vinegar

boneless chuck-eye roast + salt + black pepper > prep beef: Trim gristle, thick fat seams and silverskin. Cut into 4–5 cm cubes, pat dry and season. ~10 min
prep beef + neutral oil > brown: Set Instant Pot to Sauté (High). Brown beef in batches, then transfer to a plate. ~25 min [8-quart Instant Pot]
yellow onion + celery + garlic + tomato paste > aromatics: In the same pot cook the onion and optional celery for 5–7 minutes. Add garlic and tomato paste; cook 1 more minute. ~8 min
aromatics + dry red wine > deglaze: Add wine, scrape the bottom completely clean and simmer until reduced by half. ~5 min
brown + deglaze + low-sodium beef stock + Worcestershire sauce + MSG + dried thyme + bay leaf > pressure: Return beef with its juices. Add stock and seasonings (bay leaf optional). Seal and cook at High Pressure for 40 minutes; let pressure release naturally. If beef is still chewy, pressure cook 8–10 minutes more and release naturally. ~1 h 15 min
pressure + Yukon Gold potatoes + carrots > vegetables: Add potatoes and carrots. Sauté (Normal) uncovered, simmering 25–40 minutes and stirring occasionally until tender. Add a splash of water if needed. ~35 min
cornstarch + cold water > slurry: Whisk until smooth. [bowl]
vegetables + slurry + fresh parsley + red wine vinegar > finish: Stir in slurry gradually while simmering; cook 1–2 minutes until gravy is thick. Adjust salt, remove bay leaf and add optional parsley and vinegar to taste. ~3 min
