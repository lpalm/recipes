Rich red wine beef stew (Instant Pot)
portions 16
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
2.5 kg boneless beef shoulder roast, butcher-trimmed usable weight
produce:
1.75 kg Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into {=3 cm} chunks
900 g carrots, cut into {=3 cm} chunks
480 g yellow onion, diced
32 g garlic, minced
pantry:
55 g tomato paste
360 ml dry red wine
20 g Knorr granulated beef bouillon
40 ml Worcestershire sauce
70 g cornstarch
50 ml neutral oil
spices:
3 g MSG
3 g dried thyme
5 g salt, plus more to taste
3 g black pepper
kitchen:
1.6 l water for the bouillon
150 ml cold water for the slurry
optional:
180 g celery, diced
20 g fresh parsley, chopped

boneless beef shoulder roast + salt + black pepper > prep beef: Remove obvious exterior fat lumps, cut into {=2}–{=3} cm cubes, pat dry and season.
prep beef + neutral oil > brown: On Sauté High, brown about {=one-third} of the beef in uncrowded batches with the oil. Set aside with the remaining uncooked beef. [8-quart Instant Pot]
yellow onion + celery + garlic + tomato paste > aromatics: On Sauté Normal, cook the onion and optional celery for {=5}–{=7} minutes. Add the garlic and tomato paste and cook for {=1} minute.
aromatics + dry red wine > deglaze: Add the wine, scrape the bottom clean and press Cancel.
Knorr granulated beef bouillon + water for the bouillon > bouillon: Dissolve the granules in the water. Reserve {500 ml} for the finish.
deglaze + brown + bouillon + Worcestershire sauce + MSG + dried thyme > pressure cook: Add {1.1 l} prepared bouillon, the Worcestershire, MSG, thyme, beef and juices. Stir and cook on High Pressure for {=35} minutes. Let pressure release naturally. If the beef is still chewy, pressure-cook for {=5}–{=10} more minutes and release naturally. ~35 min
pressure cook + Yukon Gold potatoes + carrots > vegetables: Lift the beef and about {300 ml} cooking liquid into a bowl. Add the potatoes and carrots to the liquid in the pot. Cook on High Pressure for {=5} minutes and quick-release immediately. ~5 min
cornstarch + cold water for the slurry > slurry: Whisk the cornstarch and cold water together.
vegetables + slurry + fresh parsley > finish: Return the beef and its juices to the pot. Add the reserved bouillon and bring to a simmer on Sauté Normal. Stir in the slurry gradually and cook {=1}–{=2} minutes until thickened. Adjust salt, add optional parsley and serve. Makes {16 to 18} servings.
