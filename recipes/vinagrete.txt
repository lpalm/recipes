Vinagrete
portions 4
category Sides

produce:
1/2 cup onion, diced
1 cup tomatoes, cored and diced
1 cup red bell peppers, diced
1 cup green bell peppers, diced
2 tbsp cilantro, chopped
4 tbsp parsley, chopped
pantry:
1/4 cup white wine vinegar
2 tbsp olive oil

onion + tomatoes + red bell peppers + green bell peppers + cilantro + parsley + white wine vinegar + olive oil > mix: Mix in a bowl and let sit, chilled, to bring out the flavors. Salt only just before serving. [bowl]
