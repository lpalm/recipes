Crispy Cajun chickpeas
portions 2
category Other Vegan Mains

pantry:
1 can chickpeas, 15 oz, drained and towel dried
2 tbsp neutral oil
spices:
2 tbsp Cajun seasoning
salt, to taste

chickpeas + neutral oil + Cajun seasoning + salt > bake: Toss in a bowl, spread on a parchment-lined baking sheet and bake, tossing every 15 minutes, until crispy; cool about 30 minutes. ~50 min [oven 350 F]
