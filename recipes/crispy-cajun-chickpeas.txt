Crispy Cajun chickpeas
portions 2
category Other Vegan Mains

pantry:
1 can chickpeas, 15 oz, drained and towel dried
2 tbsp neutral oil
spices:
2 tbsp Cajun seasoning
salt, to taste

chickpeas + neutral oil + Cajun seasoning + salt > toss: Toss in a bowl and spread on a parchment-lined baking sheet. [baking sheet]
toss > bake: Bake, tossing every 15 minutes, until crispy. ~50 min [oven 350 F]
bake > cool: Cool about 30 minutes. ~30 min
