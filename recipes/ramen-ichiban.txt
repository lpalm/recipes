Ramen Ichiban
portions 2
category Non-vegan Mains

pantry:
2 packs instant ramen, noodles and soup packets
3 tbsp soy sauce
3 tbsp mirin
2 tbsp soy sauce for the chicken
1 tbsp mirin for the chicken
1 tsp sugar
1 tbsp neutral oil
dairy:
4 eggs
meat:
200 g boneless chicken breast
kitchen:
900 ml water
120 ml water for the chicken

eggs > boil eggs: Boil 6 to 7 minutes, cool and peel. ~7 min [small pot]
boil eggs + soy sauce + mirin > marinate eggs: Marinate the peeled eggs in the soy and mirin at least 30 minutes, up to 3 hours; halve after marinating to keep the yolk soft. ~30 min [bowl]
boneless chicken breast + soy sauce for the chicken + mirin for the chicken + sugar > marinate chicken: Marinate 20 minutes at room temperature, or up to 60 in the fridge. ~20 min [bowl]
neutral oil + marinate chicken + water for the chicken > cook chicken: Heat the oil and sear 2 minutes a side on high; add the water, cover and steam 7 to 10 minutes until 74 C inside; rest 3 minutes and slice 3 to 4 mm thick. ~12 min [pan]
water + instant ramen > noodles: Boil the water, add both noodle portions, cook 2 minutes 30 seconds, turn off the heat and stir in both soup packets. ~3 min [pot]
noodles + marinate eggs + cook chicken > bowls: Pour into bowls and top with the eggs and chicken.
