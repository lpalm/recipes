Ramen Ichiban
portions 2
category Non-vegan Mains

pantry:
2 packs instant ramen, noodles and soup packets
3 tbsp soy sauce
3 tbsp mirin
2 tbsp soy sauce for the chicken
1 tbsp mirin for the chicken
1 tsp sugar
1 tbsp neutral oil
dairy:
4 eggs
meat:
200 g boneless chicken breast
kitchen:
900 ml water
120 ml water for the chicken

eggs > boil eggs: Boil 6 to 7 minutes, cool and peel. ~7 min [small pot]
soy sauce + mirin > marinade: Mix equal parts. [bowl]
boil eggs + marinade > marinate eggs: Marinate the peeled eggs at least 30 minutes, up to 3 hours; halve after marinating to keep the yolk soft. ~30 min
boneless chicken breast + soy sauce for the chicken + mirin for the chicken + sugar > marinate chicken: Marinate 20 minutes at room temperature, or up to 60 in the fridge. ~20 min [bowl]
neutral oil + marinate chicken > sear: Heat the oil and sear 2 minutes a side on high. ~4 min [pan]
sear + water for the chicken > steam: Add the water, cover and steam until 74 C inside. ~8 min
steam > slice: Rest 3 minutes and slice 3 to 4 mm thick. ~3 min
water > boil: Bring to a boil. [pot]
boil + instant ramen > noodles: Add both noodle portions, cook 2 minutes 30 seconds, turn off the heat and stir in both soup packets. ~3 min
noodles + marinate eggs + slice > bowls: Pour into bowls and top with the eggs and chicken.
