Matcha glaze
portions 9
category Dessert

pantry:
100 g white chocolate
2 g matcha powder
crushed digestive biscuits, optional
dairy:
40 g thickened cream

white chocolate + thickened cream + matcha powder > melt: Melt together and stir smooth. [bowl]
melt + crushed digestive biscuits > dip: Dip the donuts and sprinkle with biscuit crumbs.
