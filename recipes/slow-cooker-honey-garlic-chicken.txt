Slow cooker honey garlic chicken
portions 4
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, or breasts
pantry:
1/3 cup low-sodium soy sauce
1/3 cup honey
2 tbsp tomato paste
2 tsp chili paste, sambal oelek or sriracha
1 tbsp rice vinegar
2 tbsp cornstarch
cooked brown rice, or quinoa or cauliflower rice, for serving
toasted sesame seeds, for serving
produce:
4 cloves garlic, minced
green onion, chopped, for serving

low-sodium soy sauce + honey + tomato paste + chili paste + garlic + rice vinegar > sauce: Whisk together in a large measuring cup. [measuring cup]
boneless skinless chicken thighs + sauce > slow cook: Chicken in a 6 quart slow cooker, sauce over it; cover and cook on LOW 4 to 5 hours or HIGH 2 to 3, flipping once if you can, until 165 F inside. In the Instant Pot the slow cook setting was still raw at 1 hour; pressure cooking 10 minutes on high finished it, hotter than needed. ~4 h [slow cooker]
slow cook + cornstarch > thicken: Move the chicken to a plate, whisk the cornstarch into the liquid and cook on HIGH 15 to 30 minutes until thickened, or reduce in a saucepan over medium 5 to 10 minutes. ~15 min
thicken > shred: Shred the chicken with two forks, return it and stir to coat.
shred + cooked brown rice + green onion + toasted sesame seeds > serve: Serve over the rice with green onion and sesame seeds.
