Thai-style tofu
portions 2

pantry:
16 oz super firm tofu, pressed 30 minutes, cut in 2 inch cubes
1/2 cup cornstarch
1 tbsp olive oil
1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce
2 tbsp chili garlic sauce
1 tbsp rice wine vinegar
2 tbsp brown sugar
1 tsp toasted sesame oil
1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
produce:
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 tsp ginger, grated

super firm tofu + cornstarch > coat: Coat the tofu with cornstarch in a zip bag. [zip bag]
coat + olive oil > toss: Toss with the olive oil.
toss > air fry: Air fry at 375 F, shaking after 10 minutes. ~14 min [air fryer 375 F]
low-sodium soy sauce + chili garlic sauce + rice wine vinegar + brown sugar + garlic + ginger > sauce: Mix in a skillet and bring to a boil. [skillet]
sauce + toasted sesame oil + air fry > glaze: Add the sesame oil and tofu and cook 1 minute. ~1 min
glaze + toasted sesame seeds > serve: Serve with sesame seeds.
