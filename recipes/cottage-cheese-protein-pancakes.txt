Cottage cheese protein pancakes
portions 4
category Breakfast

dairy:
2 cups cottage cheese
4 eggs
2 tbsp unsalted butter
pantry:
1 cup oat flour
2 tsp baking powder
spices:
1/2 tsp salt

cottage cheese + eggs + oat flour + baking powder + salt > batter: Blend the cottage cheese and eggs smooth, add the flour, baking powder and salt, blend again and let rest. ~10 min [blender]
batter + unsalted butter > cook: Coat a hot skillet with butter and dollop 1/3 cup of batter per pancake. Cook until golden, 2 to 3 minutes a side. [skillet]
