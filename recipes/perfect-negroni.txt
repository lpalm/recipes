Perfect Negroni
portions 1
category Cocktail

bar:
1 oz gin, Gray Whale
1 oz Campari
1/2 oz sweet vermouth, Dolin
1/2 oz dry vermouth, Dolin, a splash more than the sweet
produce:
1 slice orange

gin + Campari + sweet vermouth + dry vermouth + orange > stir: Stir with ice, strain over ice and add the slice of orange. [mixing glass]
