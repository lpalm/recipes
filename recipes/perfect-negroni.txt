Perfect Negroni
portions 1

bar:
1 oz gin, Gray Whale
1 oz Campari
1/2 oz sweet vermouth, Dolin
1/2 oz dry vermouth, Dolin, a splash more than the sweet
produce:
1 slice orange

gin + Campari + sweet vermouth + dry vermouth > stir: Stir with ice and strain over ice. [mixing glass]
stir + orange > garnish: Add the slice of orange.
