Baked pork tenderloin with chimichurri
portions 6
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
2 1/2 lb pork tenderloin
produce:
1 cup cilantro, packed
3/4 cup parsley, packed
pantry:
1 tbsp light brown sugar
1 tbsp red wine vinegar
1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
spices:
1 tbsp garlic powder
1 tbsp sea salt
1 tbsp chili powder
1/2 tsp smoked paprika
2 tsp black pepper
1/4 tsp sea salt for the sauce
1/4 tsp black pepper for the sauce
kitchen:
1/4 cup warm water

garlic powder + light brown sugar + sea salt + chili powder + smoked paprika + black pepper + pork tenderloin > coat: Combine the spices in a medium bowl, coat the tenderloin and set it on a baking sheet. [baking sheet]
coat > bake: Bake on the middle rack, flipping halfway. ~15 min [oven 400 F]
cilantro + parsley + sea salt for the sauce + black pepper for the sauce + red wine vinegar + extra virgin olive oil + warm water > chimichurri: Pulse the herbs, salt, pepper and vinegar in a food processor until chopped, then add the oil and water gradually while pulsing. [food processor]
bake + chimichurri > serve: Rest 5 minutes, slice and top with the chimichurri. ~5 min
