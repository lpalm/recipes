Tagliatelle bolognese
portions 4

1 tbsp olive oil
100 g pancetta, diced
1 onion, finely diced
1 carrot, finely diced
1 stalk celery, finely diced
400 g beef mince
200 g pork mince
150 ml whole milk
150 ml red wine
400 g canned tomatoes, crushed
2 tbsp tomato paste
1 bay leaf
1 tsp salt
1/2 tsp black pepper
3 l water
1 tbsp salt for the water
400 g tagliatelle
50 g parmesan, grated

olive oil + pancetta > render: Cook over medium heat until the fat runs and the pancetta is lightly crisp. ~5 min [large heavy pot]
render + onion + carrot + celery > soften: Add the vegetables and cook until soft and sweet without browning. ~10 min
soften + beef mince + pork mince > brown: Add the meat, break it up and cook until the liquid has cooked off and the meat sizzles. ~15 min
brown + whole milk > reduce milk: Pour in the milk and simmer until absorbed. ~10 min
reduce milk + red wine > reduce wine: Add the wine and simmer until almost gone. ~10 min
reduce wine + canned tomatoes + tomato paste + bay leaf + salt + black pepper > simmer: Stir in, bring to a bare simmer, half cover and cook on the lowest heat, stirring now and then. Add a splash of water if it dries out. ~2 h
water + salt for the water > boil: Bring to a boil in a large pot. ~10 min [large pot]
boil + tagliatelle > cook: Cook until al dente, drain and keep a cup of the water. ~8 min
simmer + cook > toss: Toss the pasta with the sauce and a splash of pasta water.
toss + parmesan > serve: Serve with the parmesan on top.
