Peanut chickpeas
portions 2

pantry:
1/3 cup peanut butter
1/4 cup soy sauce
1 1/2 tbsp sugar, or maple syrup
1/2 tbsp oil
1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
produce:
2 tsp garlic, minced
2 tbsp lime juice
1/3 cup onion, chopped
1 tsp garlic for the pan, minced
2 cups spinach
spices:
1 1/2 tsp ginger powder, or fresh minced ginger
2 tsp chili flakes
2 tsp paprika
1 tsp cinnamon
kitchen:
2 tbsp water, up to 6

peanut butter + soy sauce + ginger powder + garlic + chili flakes + lime juice + sugar > paste: Combine in a small bowl. [bowl]
paste + water > peanut sauce: Mix in the water a tbsp at a time to the consistency you like; rest at least 10 minutes. The sauce is about twice what one can needs. ~10 min
oil + onion > onions: Heat the oil in a large pan over medium and sauté the onion until it starts to brown. ~5 min [large pan]
onions + garlic for the pan > fragrant: Mix in the garlic and sauté until it starts to brown.
fragrant + spinach > wilt: Mix in the spinach and sauté until small and wilted.
wilt + chickpeas > heat: Add the chickpeas and heat through. ~10 min
heat + paprika + cinnamon > spice: Add the spices late so they don't burn.
spice + peanut sauce > finish: Pour in the peanut sauce, stir, turn to low and cook until everything is warm. ~5 min
