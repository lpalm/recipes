Peanut chickpeas
portions 2
category Other Vegan Mains

pantry:
1/3 cup peanut butter
1/4 cup soy sauce
1 1/2 tbsp sugar, or maple syrup
1/2 tbsp oil
1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
produce:
2 tsp garlic, minced
2 tbsp lime juice
1/3 cup onion, chopped
1 tsp garlic for the pan, minced
2 cups spinach
spices:
1 1/2 tsp ginger powder, or fresh minced ginger
2 tsp chili flakes
2 tsp paprika
1 tsp cinnamon
kitchen:
2 tbsp water, up to 6

peanut butter + soy sauce + ginger powder + garlic + chili flakes + lime juice + sugar + water > peanut sauce: Combine in a small bowl, mixing in the water a tbsp at a time to the consistency you like; rest at least 10 minutes. ~10 min [bowl]
oil + onion + garlic for the pan + spinach > sauté: Heat the oil in a large pan over medium and sauté the onion until it starts to brown, about 5 minutes; mix in the garlic until it starts to brown, then the spinach until small and wilted. ~7 min [large pan]
sauté + chickpeas + paprika + cinnamon > heat: Add the chickpeas and heat about 10 minutes, adding the spices late so they don't burn. ~10 min
heat + peanut sauce > finish: Pour in the peanut sauce, stir, turn to low and cook until everything is warm. ~5 min
