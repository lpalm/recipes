Cheese fondue
portions 2
category Non-vegan Mains

dairy:
200 g Gruyère, or Comté
150 g Fontina, or Havarti
50 g sharp white Cheddar
bakery:
300 g baguette, cut in 2 cm cubes and left out 6 to 12 hours
bar:
150 ml Sauvignon Blanc
15 g brandy, or kirsch
produce:
1 clove garlic, halved
pantry:
5 g cornstarch
spices:
1 g black pepper, freshly ground
0.3 g nutmeg, ground

Gruyère + Fontina + sharp white Cheddar > grate: Grate and mix in a bowl. [bowl]
garlic > rub: Rub the pot with the cut garlic. [fondue pot]
rub + Sauvignon Blanc > heat: Add the wine and bring to 80 to 85 C, steaming but not boiling.
heat + grate > melt: Stir in the cheese by handfuls in a figure 8, waiting for each to melt before the next.
cornstarch + brandy > slurry: Dissolve the cornstarch in the brandy. [cup]
melt + slurry > thicken: Add the slurry and stir 1 to 2 minutes until glossy and lightly thickened.
thicken + black pepper + nutmeg > season: Stir in the pepper and nutmeg.
season + baguette > serve: Serve at once with the bread, keeping the pot at 55 to 60 C.
