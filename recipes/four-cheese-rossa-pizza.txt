Four cheese rossa pizza
portions 1
category Pizza Making

bakery:
250 g Neapolitan pizza dough
pantry:
80 g tomato sauce, San Marzano or good crushed tomatoes
1 tsp olive oil
dairy:
60 g fior di latte mozzarella
15 g Gorgonzola dolce, or Roquefort
15 g Fontina, or Taleggio
15 g Parmigiano Reggiano
produce:
3 leaves fresh basil

Neapolitan pizza dough + tomato sauce + fior di latte mozzarella + Gorgonzola dolce + Fontina + Parmigiano Reggiano + fresh basil > bake: Stretch, sauce, the four cheeses and the basil, and bake in the Ooni at about 80% of its maximum. ~2 min [Ooni]
bake + olive oil > finish: Drizzle with oil.
