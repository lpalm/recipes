Open-face croissant cheese dreams
portions 6
category Sides

dairy:
175 g medium cheddar, shredded
1/2 cup unsalted butter, soft
1 tbsp heavy cream
1 large egg
pantry:
1/2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
2 tbsp Frank's Buffalo sauce
bakery:
6 slices croissant loaf, thick
spices:
1/4 tsp table salt
1/4 tsp dry mustard

medium cheddar + unsalted butter + heavy cream + large egg + Worcestershire sauce + table salt + dry mustard + Frank's Buffalo sauce > cheese mix: Beat the butter and cheese smooth, then beat in the rest. [mixer]
cheese mix + croissant loaf > spread: Spread about 25 g (2 tbsp) of cheese mix on each slice; flash-freeze 10 minutes to anchor the cheese if you like.
spread > air fry: Air fry at 350 F, spinning the basket once halfway, until the tops are deep gold and bubbling. From the fridge 5 to 7 minutes, frozen 8 to 10. ~5 min [air fryer 350 F]
