General Tso's tofu
portions 2
category Tofu Vegan Mains

pantry:
10 oz firm tofu, cubed
2 tbsp tamari, or soy sauce
1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
6 tbsp cornstarch
oil, for frying
3 tbsp tamari for the sauce
3 tbsp apple cider vinegar for the sauce
3 tbsp vegetable stock, or water
3 tbsp brown sugar
1 tbsp cornstarch for the sauce
cooked white rice, for serving
sesame seeds and chopped chives, for garnish, optional
produce:
2 cloves garlic, minced
spices:
1/8 tsp red pepper flakes, optional

firm tofu + tamari + apple cider vinegar > marinate: Stir together in a shallow dish and rest at least 5 minutes, preferably overnight; drain and discard the marinade. ~5 min [shallow dish]
marinate + cornstarch + oil > fry: Toss the tofu with the cornstarch in a bag, a third at a time; heat some oil in a skillet and cook over medium-high until golden on all sides; set aside. [skillet]
garlic > brown garlic: In the skillet, with more oil if needed, cook over medium-high, stirring, until golden brown; set aside. ~2 min
brown garlic + tamari for the sauce + apple cider vinegar for the sauce + vegetable stock + brown sugar + cornstarch for the sauce + red pepper flakes > thicken: Mix the sauce in a bowl, add it to the skillet with the garlic and cook over medium-high, stirring, until it thickens.
thicken + fry + cooked white rice + sesame seeds and chopped chives > glaze: Add the tofu, stir and cook 1 to 2 minutes more; serve over rice with the garnish. Keeps 5 days. ~2 min
