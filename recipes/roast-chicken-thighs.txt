Roast chicken thighs with potatoes
portions 2

600 g potatoes, cut in 3 cm chunks
2 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp salt
1/2 tsp black pepper
1 tsp smoked paprika
4 chicken thighs, bone in and skin on
3 cloves garlic, smashed
2 sprigs rosemary
1 lemon, halved
1/2 tsp flaky salt

potatoes + olive oil + salt + black pepper + smoked paprika > toss: Toss in the tin until every piece is coated. [roasting tin]
toss + chicken thighs + garlic + rosemary + lemon + flaky salt > arrange: Nestle the thighs skin up among the potatoes with the garlic and rosemary, lemon halves cut side down. Sprinkle the skin with the flaky salt.
arrange > roast: Roast until the skin is deep golden and the potatoes are crisp, turning the potatoes once halfway. ~45 min [oven 220 fan]
roast > rest: Rest, then squeeze the roasted lemon over everything. ~5 min
