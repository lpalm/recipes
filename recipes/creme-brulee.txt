Crème brûlée
portions 4
category Dessert

dairy:
500 g heavy cream
85 g egg yolk
pantry:
50 g caster sugar
10 g vanilla extract
24 g white sugar for the top
spices:
0.4 g fine salt
kitchen:
hot water, 75 to 82 C, enough to come halfway up the ramekins

heavy cream + fine salt > heat: Heat to 70 to 75 C, then take off the heat. [saucepan]
egg yolk + caster sugar > whisk: Whisk gently together. [mixing bowl]
heat + whisk + vanilla extract > temper: Pour the hot cream slowly into the yolks while whisking, then mix in the vanilla.
temper > strain: Strain through a fine sieve into a jug and divide between 4 shallow ramekins set in a deep baking dish. [fine sieve]
strain + hot water > bath: Put the dish on the partly pulled-out oven rack and pour the hot water around the ramekins to halfway up their sides.
bath > bake: Bake until the centre of one custard reaches 77 to 79 C; start checking after 25 minutes. ~35 min [oven 130]
bake > chill: Take the ramekins out of the water, cool 1 hour at room temperature, then refrigerate at least 4 hours, preferably overnight. ~4 h
chill + white sugar for the top > torch: Blot any condensation, sprinkle 6 g of sugar over each custard and torch, keeping the flame moving, until golden amber. Serve at once. [kitchen torch]
