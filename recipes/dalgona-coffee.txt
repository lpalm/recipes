Dalgona coffee
portions 1
category Drinks

pantry:
6 g decaf instant coffee
12 g sugar
dairy:
full fat milk, cow or oat
kitchen:
15 g hot water
ice

decaf instant coffee + sugar + hot water > whip: Whisk until beige and fluffy. [bowl]
whip + full fat milk + ice > pour: Pour the milk into a glass, add ice and spoon the coffee foam on top.
