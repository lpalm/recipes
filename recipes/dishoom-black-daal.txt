Dishoom black daal
portions 8
category Non-vegan Mains

pantry:
800 g whole black urad dal
187 g tomato purée
produce:
32 g garlic paste
27 g ginger paste
dairy:
240 g unsalted butter
240 ml double cream
spices:
16 g salt
5 g deggi mirch chili powder
3 g garam masala, try 5
kitchen:
4 l water

whole black urad dal > rinse: Cover with water in a large bowl, whisk 10 seconds, let settle and pour off; repeat 3 or 4 times until the water runs clear. [bowl]
rinse + water > boil: Tip into the instant pot with the water and pressure cook on high, skimming the scum, 30 minutes, then 30 minutes more, skimming again. ~1 h [instant pot]
boil > drain: Pour off the cooking water and top up with fresh water to cover the daal by 3 to 4 cm.
garlic paste + ginger paste > paste: Blend with 1/4 cup of water into a fine paste. [blender]
drain + paste + tomato purée + salt + deggi mirch chili powder + unsalted butter + garam masala > season: Add and stir until well blended.
season > pressure: Pressure cook on high, 1 hour, try 3. ~1 h
pressure + double cream > finish: Add the cream and cook 10 minutes more; it should be very thick. ~10 min
