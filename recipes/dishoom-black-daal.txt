Dishoom black daal
portions 8
category Non-vegan Mains

pantry:
800 g whole black urad dal
187 g tomato purée
produce:
32 g garlic paste
27 g ginger paste
dairy:
240 g unsalted butter
240 ml double cream
spices:
16 g salt
5 g deggi mirch chili powder
3 g garam masala
kitchen:
4 l water

whole black urad dal + water > boil: Rinse in a bowl, whisking, until the water runs clear; tip into the instant pot with the water and pressure cook on high, skimming the scum, 30 minutes, then 30 minutes more, skimming again. ~1 h [instant pot]
boil + garlic paste + ginger paste + tomato purée + salt + deggi mirch chili powder + unsalted butter + garam masala > season: Pour off the cooking water and top up with fresh water to cover the daal by 3 to 4 cm; blend the garlic and ginger with 1/4 cup of water, add with the rest and stir until well blended.
season > pressure: Pressure cook on high. ~1 h
pressure + double cream > finish: Add the cream and cook 10 minutes more; it should be very thick. ~10 min
