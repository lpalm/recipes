Rice pilaf
portions 6
category Sides

pantry:
2 2/3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
2 tbsp olive oil
1/3 cup vermicelli, or orzo
1 cup long grain white rice
dairy:
1/3 cup unsalted butter
produce:
1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
spices:
1 tsp kosher salt
1/2 tsp garlic powder
1/4 tsp ground black pepper
1/8 tsp paprika
1 pinch onion powder

low-sodium chicken broth + unsalted butter + kosher salt + garlic powder + ground black pepper + paprika + onion powder > broth: Heat in a saucepan over medium until it simmers, then keep on low. [saucepan]
olive oil + vermicelli > brown: Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-low and cook the vermicelli until brown. ~5 min [large skillet]
brown + long grain white rice > toast: Add the rice and cook until it turns bright white. ~6 min
toast + broth > simmer: Pour in the warm broth, stir, and once it simmers cover and cook. ~15 min
simmer + fresh parsley > steam: Add the parsley, cover and cook until the broth is absorbed, then fluff with a fork. ~5 min
