Gingerbread cocktail
portions 1

bar:
1 oz ginger liqueur, Giffard Ginger of the Indies
1 oz blackstrap rum, Cruzan
1/2 oz allspice dram, St. Elizabeth or homemade
2 dashes tiki bitters
1 oz milk stout, Keegan's or Left Hand nitro
pantry:
cinnamon sugar

ginger liqueur + blackstrap rum + allspice dram + tiki bitters > shake: Shake hard with ice and strain into a wine goblet. [shaker]
shake + milk stout > top: Top with the milk stout.
top + cinnamon sugar > dust: Dust with cinnamon sugar.
