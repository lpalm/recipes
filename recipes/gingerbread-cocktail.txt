Gingerbread cocktail
portions 1
category Cocktail

bar:
1 oz ginger liqueur, Giffard Ginger of the Indies
1 oz blackstrap rum, Cruzan
1/2 oz allspice dram, St. Elizabeth or homemade
2 dashes tiki bitters
1 oz milk stout, Keegan's or Left Hand nitro
pantry:
cinnamon sugar

ginger liqueur + blackstrap rum + allspice dram + tiki bitters + milk stout + cinnamon sugar > shake: Shake hard with ice, strain into a wine goblet, top with the milk stout and dust with cinnamon sugar. [shaker]
