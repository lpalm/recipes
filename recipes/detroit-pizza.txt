Detroit pizza
portions 2
category Pizza Making

pantry:
160 g bread flour
0.6 g active dry yeast
0.4 g low diastatic malt
5 g olive oil
14 g avocado oil, for the pan
100 g crushed tomatoes
2 g olive oil for the sauce
pepperoni, enough to cover the surface
dairy:
110 g low-moisture mozzarella
92 g Muenster, or Jack
32 g Parmesan
spices:
3.2 g salt
1 g salt for the sauce
0.8 g garlic powder
0.5 g black pepper
0.4 g oregano
0.4 g Italian seasoning
oregano for the dough, a sprinkle
kitchen:
118 g water, 100 to 105 F

water + active dry yeast > bloom: Water at 100 to 105 F in a bowl, add the yeast and stir briefly. [bowl]
bloom + bread flour + salt + low diastatic malt + olive oil > mix: Add and mix 4 to 5 minutes, then rest 25 minutes in the bowl. ~25 min
mix > fold: Do the stretch-and-fold.
avocado oil > pan: Oil the 8 by 10 pan. [8x10 pan]
fold + pan > rest: Move the dough to the pan right after the fold, cover and rest 30 to 45 minutes. ~40 min
rest > press: Press partway toward the edges, rest 15 to 20 minutes, then press fully into the corners. ~18 min
press > proof: Same day: proof 60 to 90 minutes at room temperature until puffy, not doubled. Overnight: refrigerate 12 to 16 hours, rest 60 minutes, press to the corners if needed. ~1 h 15 min
crushed tomatoes + salt for the sauce + garlic powder + black pepper + oregano + Italian seasoning + olive oil for the sauce > sauce: Mix. [bowl]
proof + oregano for the dough + pepperoni > top: Sprinkle oregano over the dough and cover the surface with pepperoni.
top + low-moisture mozzarella + Muenster + Parmesan > cheese: Mozzarella and Jack against the pan edges, Parmesan slightly inward.
cheese + sauce > stripe: 2 or 3 stripes of sauce over the top.
stripe > bake: Ooni preheated 35 to 45 minutes on medium, stone centre 575 to 600 F; flame to lowest before inserting the pan. Bake, rest 3 to 5 minutes, lift onto a rack, slice. ~8 min [Ooni Koda]
