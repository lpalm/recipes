Butternut squash soup
portions 4
category Sides

produce:
1 small onion, peeled and quartered
1/2 apple, peeled, cored and quartered
1 small carrot, peeled and quartered
460 g butternut squash, peeled and cut in 2.5 cm pieces
pantry:
2 tbsp olive oil
120 g raw cashews
750 ml hot vegetable stock
spices:
1 tsp salt
1 tsp dried thyme leaves

olive oil + small onion + salt > chop: Put in the jug, select CHOP, then SAUTE. [Ninja blender]
chop + raw cashews + apple + small carrot + butternut squash + dried thyme leaves + hot vegetable stock > soup: Add the rest and select SMOOTH SOUP.
