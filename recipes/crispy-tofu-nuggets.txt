Crispy tofu nuggets
portions 2
category Tofu Vegan Mains

pantry:
2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for the pan
2 tbsp all-purpose flour, or rice flour
2 tbsp cornstarch, or potato starch
2 tbsp nutritional yeast
14 oz firm tofu, drained
condiment of choice, for serving
spices:
1 tbsp garlic powder
1 tbsp onion powder
1 1/2 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt, or 3/4 tsp Morton

all-purpose flour + cornstarch + nutritional yeast + garlic powder + onion powder + Diamond Crystal kosher salt > seasoning: Whisk together in a medium bowl. [bowl]
firm tofu + extra-virgin olive oil + seasoning > coat: Pat the tofu dry, break into craggy nugget-size pieces about 1 inch and pat dry again; toss with half the oil, sprinkle on the seasoning and toss, then drizzle the rest of the oil and toss again; spread on an oiled baking sheet, touching the pan. [baking sheet]
coat + condiment of choice > bake: Bake on the upper third rack until golden brown and crisp, dropping to 400 F halfway if browning too fast; season with salt if you like and serve with the condiment. ~32 min [oven 425 F]
