Cold brew
portions 8
category Breakfast

pantry:
350 g coffee beans, ground coarse at {=10} (mocca)
kitchen:
8 cups water

coffee beans + water > steep: Put the coffee into the paper filter bag in the Toddy maker and pour the measured water over the grounds. Steep at room temperature, then drain into the glass jug. For larger quantities, split the coffee and water proportionally across separate batches. ~18 h [Toddy maker]
