Cold brew
portions 8

pantry:
350 g coffee beans, ground coarse at 10 (mocca)
kitchen:
water

coffee beans > fill: Put the coffee into the paper filter bag in the Toddy maker. [Toddy maker]
fill + water > steep: Pour water over the coffee in the open filter bag until the top is half full and steep at room temperature. ~18 h
steep > drain: Drain into the glass jug.
