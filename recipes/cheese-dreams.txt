Cheese dreams
portions 8
category Sides

dairy:
2 cups sharp Cheddar, finely grated from a block
1 cup unsalted butter, softened
2 tbsp heavy cream
1 large egg
pantry:
1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
hot sauce, to taste, Frank's Buffalo
bakery:
16 oz firm white sandwich bread, or croissant loaf
spices:
1/2 tsp table salt
1/2 tsp dry mustard

sharp Cheddar + unsalted butter > beat: Beat at medium speed with an electric mixer until blended. [mixer]
beat + heavy cream + large egg + Worcestershire sauce + table salt + dry mustard + hot sauce > cheese mix: Beat in the rest.
cheese mix + firm white sandwich bread > build: Cut off the crusts, cut each slice in 4 squares, spread cheese mix on half of them (about 1 tsp each), top with the rest and spread the remaining mix over the tops and sides. Set 1 inch apart on a greased baking sheet. [baking sheet]
build > bake: Bake until golden brown; from frozen add 10 minutes. ~15 min [oven 375 F]
