Strawberry white chocolate glaze
portions 9
category Dessert

pantry:
100 g white chocolate
crushed digestive biscuits, optional
produce:
50 g fresh strawberries, crushed

white chocolate + fresh strawberries > melt: Melt the chocolate with the strawberries and stir smooth. [bowl]
melt + crushed digestive biscuits > dip: Dip the donuts and sprinkle with biscuit crumbs.
