Refried black beans
portions 2

pantry:
1 can refried black beans
1/2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
2 tbsp olive oil
spices:
1 tbsp taco mix
1/2 tbsp dried minced onion
1/2 tsp garlic powder
1/2 tbsp ancho chili powder

refried black beans + apple cider vinegar + taco mix + dried minced onion + olive oil + garlic powder + ancho chili powder > warm: Stir everything together in a pan and warm through. [pan]
