Mudslide
portions 1
category Cocktail

bar:
1 oz vodka
1 oz Baileys
1 oz Kahlúa
dairy:
1/2 oz double cream
pantry:
1/2 tbsp cocoa powder
chocolate twirls, optional
kitchen:
3 oz ice

vodka + Baileys + Kahlúa + double cream + cocoa powder + ice + chocolate twirls > blend: Blend until the ice is small and dress with a few chocolate twirls. [blender]
