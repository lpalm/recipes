Neapolitan pizza dough
portions 1
category Pizza Making

pantry:
160 g 00 flour
0.2 g active dry yeast
3 g olive oil
sugar, a pinch
spices:
4.5 g salt
kitchen:
98 g water, warm

active dry yeast + sugar + water + salt + 00 flour + olive oil > mix: Foam the yeast in a splash of the warm water with a pinch of sugar 5 to 10 minutes; dissolve the salt in the rest of the water, add the yeast, all the flour and the oil and mix by hand until shaggy and uniform. ~8 min [bowl]
mix > knead: Knead about 5 minutes until tacky but cohesive and rest covered 7 to 10 minutes; stretch and fold 4 to 5 times, rest 7 to 10 minutes, and repeat once or twice until smooth. ~35 min
knead > bulk: Bulk ferment in a container at about 65 F; the dough should barely rise. ~24 h
bulk > ball: Divide into 250 g balls in oiled containers and ferment 18 hours at about 65 F, then 4 to 6 hours at about 78 F, until soft, puffy and smooth. ~22 h
