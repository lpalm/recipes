Neapolitan pizza dough
portions 1

pantry:
160 g 00 flour
0.2 g active dry yeast
3 g olive oil
sugar, a pinch
spices:
4.5 g salt
kitchen:
98 g water, warm

active dry yeast + sugar > bloom: Mix the yeast with a splash of the warm water and a pinch of sugar; let foam 5 to 10 minutes. ~8 min [cup]
water + salt > brine: Dissolve the salt in the remaining water. [bowl]
brine + bloom + 00 flour + olive oil > mix: Add the yeast mixture and all the flour and mix by hand until shaggy and uniform.
mix > knead: Knead about 5 minutes until tacky but cohesive, then rest covered 7 to 10 minutes. ~15 min
knead > fold: Stretch and fold 4 to 5 times, rest 7 to 10 minutes, and repeat once or twice until smooth. ~20 min
fold > bulk: Bulk ferment in a container at about 65 F; the dough should barely rise. ~24 h
bulk > ball: Divide into 250 g balls in oiled containers and ferment 18 hours at about 65 F, then 4 to 6 hours at about 78 F, until soft, puffy and smooth. ~22 h
