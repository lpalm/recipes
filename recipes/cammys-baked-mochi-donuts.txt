Cammy's baked mochi donuts
portions 12

pantry:
100 g mochiko sweet rice flour
1/2 tsp baking powder
60 g sugar
dairy:
28 g butter, melted
2 egg yolks
1/2 cup milk
spices:
1/4 tsp kosher salt

mochiko sweet rice flour + baking powder + kosher salt > dry mix: Whisk together in a medium bowl. [bowl]
butter + sugar > cream: Mix together in a separate bowl. [bowl]
cream + egg yolks + milk > wet mix: Add the yolks, combine, then the milk. For the ube version add 2 to 3 tbsp ube halaya jam and 1/2 tsp ube extract here.
dry mix + wet mix > batter: Mix the liquid into the dry ingredients until just combined.
batter > bake: Fill an oiled mini donut pan 3/4 full and bake until the outside is golden and crisp and the inside light and chewy. ~15 min [oven 350 F] [mini donut pan]
