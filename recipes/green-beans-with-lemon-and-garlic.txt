Green beans with lemon and garlic
portions 6
category Sides

produce:
2 lb green beans, ends trimmed
6 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp lemon zest
pantry:
1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
dairy:
3 tbsp butter
spices:
1 tsp red pepper flakes
salt and freshly ground black pepper
kitchen:
salted water
ice water

green beans + salted water > blanch: Blanch in well-salted boiling water until bright green and tender-crisp. ~2 min [stock pot]
blanch + ice water > shock: Drain and shock in ice water.
extra virgin olive oil + butter + garlic + red pepper flakes > sizzle: Heat a large heavy skillet over medium, add the oil and butter, then the garlic and pepper flakes; sauté until fragrant, about 30 seconds. [heavy skillet]
sizzle + shock > toss: Add the beans and sauté until coated and heated through. ~5 min
toss + lemon zest + salt and freshly ground black pepper > season: Add the lemon zest and season.
