Red lentil sloppy joes
portions 14
category Other Vegan Mains

produce:
1 large sweet onion, chopped
1 medium green pepper, chopped
1/2 medium sweet red pepper, chopped
1 medium carrot, shredded
6 cloves garlic, minced
5 plum tomatoes, chopped
pantry:
2 tbsp olive oil
2 1/2 cups reduced-sodium vegetable broth
1 cup dried red lentils, rinsed
1 can tomato sauce, 8 oz
2 tbsp yellow mustard
4 1/2 tsp cider vinegar
2 tsp vegan Worcestershire sauce
2 tsp honey
1 1/2 tsp tomato paste
bakery:
14 whole wheat hamburger buns, split and toasted
spices:
2 tbsp chili powder
1/4 tsp salt
1/8 tsp pepper

olive oil + large sweet onion + medium green pepper + medium sweet red pepper + medium carrot > sauté: Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high and cook, stirring, until crisp-tender. ~7 min [large skillet]
sauté + garlic > fragrant: Add the garlic and cook 1 minute. ~1 min
fragrant + reduced-sodium vegetable broth + dried red lentils > simmer: Add, bring to a boil, then simmer uncovered, stirring now and then, until the lentils are tender. ~15 min
simmer + plum tomatoes + tomato sauce + chili powder + yellow mustard + cider vinegar + vegan Worcestershire sauce + honey + tomato paste + salt + pepper > thicken: Stir in, bring to a boil, then simmer until thickened. ~10 min
thicken + whole wheat hamburger buns > serve: Serve on the buns.
