Mozzarella sticks
portions 4

dairy:
6 string cheese sticks, cold, cut crosswise in half
pantry:
12 egg roll wrappers
1 tbsp vegetable oil
spicy marinara sauce, for dipping
spices:
1 tsp dried basil
kosher salt

string cheese sticks + egg roll wrappers + dried basil > wrap: Lay half a stick diagonally on a wrapper, sprinkle with basil, roll halfway, fold the sides in, roll to the end and seal the edge with a wet finger.
wrap + vegetable oil + kosher salt > freeze: Set on the air fry tray not touching, brush with oil, sprinkle with salt and freeze. ~15 min [air fry tray]
freeze > fry: Air fry at 375 F on the highest rack, turning once, until lightly browned and crisp. ~5 min [air fryer 375 F]
fry + spicy marinara sauce > serve: Serve hot with the marinara.
