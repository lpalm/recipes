Roasted broccoli with Parmesan and lemon
portions 4
category Sides

produce:
1 1/2 lb broccoli, cut in even florets
1 tbsp lemon juice, about half a lemon
3 cloves garlic, minced
pantry:
3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
dairy:
1/4 cup Parmesan, grated
spices:
kosher salt
freshly ground black pepper

broccoli + garlic + extra virgin olive oil + lemon juice + kosher salt > toss: Toss until lightly coated, then spread in a single layer on an oiled or lined baking sheet. [baking sheet]
toss > roast: Roast, flipping halfway, until fork tender and lightly browned; some charring is good. ~18 min [oven 425 F]
roast + Parmesan + freshly ground black pepper > finish: Back in the bowl, toss with lots of black pepper and the Parmesan. Serve at once.
