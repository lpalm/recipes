Chili
portions 10
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
2 lb ground turkey, 99% fat free
produce:
2 large yellow onions, diced
2 red bell peppers, diced
pantry:
oil for the pan
4 cubes Knorr beef bouillon, or 15 g powder
1 l chicken broth, less salt
14 oz canned fire-roasted green chiles, drained
30 oz canned kidney beans, no salt, rinsed and drained
30 oz canned black beans, no salt, rinsed and drained
30 oz canned fire-roasted diced tomatoes, undrained
2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
6 oz tomato paste
1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa
2 tbsp dark brown sugar
1/2 tsp instant coffee
spices:
2 tbsp garlic powder
1 tbsp Mexene chili powder
1 tbsp ancho chili powder
1 tsp cumin powder
2 tsp smoked paprika
1 tsp ground black pepper
1/4 tsp cayenne pepper

ground turkey > cook meat: Cook the meat, adding a little oil if very lean, breaking it into golf-ball chunks; once cooked but not browned, drain the fat and set aside. [instant pot]
oil for the pan + large yellow onions > onions: Cook the onions in a little oil until the water is gone and the aromas come out. ~15 min
onions + cook meat + garlic powder + Mexene chili powder + ancho chili powder + cumin powder + smoked paprika + ground black pepper + cayenne pepper > spice: Add the meat and all the spices, stir well and cook until the meat browns. ~8 min
spice + red bell peppers + canned fire-roasted green chiles + chicken broth + Knorr beef bouillon > pressure: Add the peppers, chiles, broth and bouillon and pressure cook on high. ~50 min
pressure + canned kidney beans + canned black beans + canned fire-roasted diced tomatoes + tomato paste + unsweetened cocoa + apple cider vinegar + instant coffee + dark brown sugar + Worcestershire sauce > finish: Add everything else, stir well and pressure cook on high. Serve with sour cream, shredded cheddar and corn chips. ~10 min
