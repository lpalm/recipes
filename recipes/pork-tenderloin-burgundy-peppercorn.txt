Pork tenderloin with burgundy peppercorn sauce
portions 4
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
1 lb pork tenderloin, trimmed of fat and silver skin
produce:
1 cup shallots, finely chopped, or onion
3 cloves garlic, minced
1/2 cup parsley, chopped
pantry:
1 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp olive oil for the sauce
1/2 cup red wine
2 cups beef broth
dairy:
1/2 cup heavy cream
spices:
1/4 cup black peppercorns, freshly and coarsely cracked
salt

pork tenderloin + black peppercorns > crust: Season the tenderloin generously with the cracked pepper, pressing so it sticks; keep up to 1 tbsp for the sauce.
olive oil + crust > sear: Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high and brown on all sides, about 1 minute a side. ~5 min [large skillet]
sear > roast: Move to a greased baking dish and roast until 140 F in the thickest part; rest loosely covered with foil. ~18 min [oven 425 F]
olive oil for the sauce + shallots > soften: Meanwhile sauté the shallots in a saucepan over medium-high. ~2 min [saucepan]
soften + garlic > aromatics: Add the garlic and sauté 1 minute more. ~1 min
aromatics + red wine > deglaze: Add the wine and scrape up the browned bits as it boils; cook until almost gone.
deglaze + beef broth > reduce: Add the broth, turn to high and boil down until a spoon dragged through leaves a trail. ~5 min
reduce + heavy cream > cream: Pour in the cream and boil down again until the spoon leaves a trail.
cream + parsley + salt > finish: Off the heat add the parsley and any remaining cracked pepper, up to 1 tbsp; taste for salt.
roast + finish > serve: Slice the tenderloin on a slight diagonal, 1/2 to 1 inch thick, and spoon the sauce over.
