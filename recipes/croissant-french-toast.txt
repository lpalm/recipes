Croissant French toast
portions 2

dairy:
2 eggs
60 ml milk, or heavy cream
butter for the pan
pantry:
1/2 tbsp sugar, or maple syrup
1 tsp vanilla extract
bakery:
4 slices croissant bread
spices:
1/4 tsp cinnamon

eggs + milk + sugar + vanilla extract + cinnamon > whisk: Whisk until smooth. [bowl]
whisk + croissant bread > dip: Dip each slice into the egg mixture, coating both sides.
dip + butter for the pan > fry: Fry in a lightly buttered non-stick pan over medium heat until golden, 3 to 5 minutes a side. [non-stick pan]
