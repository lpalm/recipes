Couve for feijoada
portions 2
category Sides

produce:
200 g collard greens, stems removed, sliced very thin
8 g garlic, minced
8 ml lime juice
pantry:
18 ml olive oil
spices:
0.7 g salt

olive oil + garlic > sizzle: Heat a large pan on medium-high for 90 seconds, add the oil, then the garlic; cook 25 seconds, stirring. [large pan]
sizzle + collard greens + salt + lime juice > wilt: Add the greens and salt and cook 3 minutes, tossing continuously; turn off the heat and mix in the lime juice. ~3 min
