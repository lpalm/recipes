Pizza diavola
portions 1
category Pizza Making

bakery:
250 g Neapolitan pizza dough
pantry:
80 g tomato sauce
5 g olive oil
1 tsp Calabrian chili oil, or chili flakes
dairy:
80 g mozzarella, torn small
meat:
30 g spicy soppressata, up to {40 g}; {4 to 6} thin slices
produce:
3 leaves fresh basil, up to {4 leaves}

Neapolitan pizza dough + tomato sauce + mozzarella + spicy soppressata + olive oil > bake: Make a dough ball for each portion. Stretch each and distribute the toppings: spread the sauce leaving a {=1} cm border, mozzarella sparingly, the soppressata, a drizzle of oil, and bake in the Ooni at about {=80}% of its maximum. ~2 min [Ooni]
bake + Calabrian chili oil + fresh basil > finish: Drizzle with the chili oil and add the basil.
