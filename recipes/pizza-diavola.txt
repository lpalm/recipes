Pizza diavola
portions 1

bakery:
250 g Neapolitan pizza dough
pantry:
80 g tomato sauce
5 g olive oil
1 tsp Calabrian chili oil, or chili flakes
dairy:
80 g mozzarella, torn small
meat:
35 g spicy soppressata, 4 to 6 thin slices
produce:
4 leaves fresh basil

Neapolitan pizza dough + tomato sauce + mozzarella + spicy soppressata + olive oil > build: Spread the sauce leaving a 1 cm border, mozzarella sparingly, the soppressata, a drizzle of oil.
build > bake: Bake in the Ooni at about 80% of its maximum. ~2 min [Ooni]
bake + Calabrian chili oil + fresh basil > finish: Drizzle with the chili oil and add the basil.
