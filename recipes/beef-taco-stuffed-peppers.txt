Beef taco stuffed peppers
portions 4
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
1 lb lean ground beef
produce:
1 small onion, chopped
1 clove garlic, minced
4 large bell peppers, any color
pantry:
1 can tomatoes with green chiles, 10 oz
1/2 cup crushed tortilla chips
vegetable cooking spray
dairy:
1/3 cup queso, crumbled, or shredded Jack cheese
spices:
1 tbsp chili powder
2 tsp ground cumin
1/2 tsp kosher salt
1/4 tsp black pepper

lean ground beef + small onion + garlic > brown: Cook in a skillet over medium-high, stirring to break up, until no longer pink. [skillet]
brown + chili powder + ground cumin + kosher salt + black pepper > spice: Stir in the spices.
spice + tomatoes with green chiles > simmer: Stir in the tomatoes and the chopped pepper tops, drop to medium and cook. ~5 min
large bell peppers > hollow: Cut off the tops and chop them for the beef; discard seeds and membranes and stand the peppers in the sprayed air fry basket. [air fry basket]
hollow + simmer > fill: Spoon the beef mixture into the peppers.
fill + crushed tortilla chips + queso + vegetable cooking spray > top: Top each with the chips and cheese and spray with cooking spray.
top > air fry: Air fry at 350 F until the peppers are fork-tender and the filling hot. ~20 min [air fryer 350 F]
