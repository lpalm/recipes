Red lentil dhal
portions 16

pantry:
4 tbsp olive oil
4 cups dried red lentils, rinsed and drained
4 cans chopped tomatoes, 400 ml each
4 cans coconut milk, 400 ml each
5 cubes vegetable stock
produce:
8 yellow onions, diced
16 cloves garlic, minced
4 tbsp fresh ginger, peeled and grated
spices:
1 tsp dried chili flakes
2 tsp cumin seeds
2 tsp coriander seeds
2 tsp mustard seeds
3 tbsp ground turmeric
2 tbsp garam masala
1 tsp black pepper

olive oil + yellow onions > caramelize: In the instant pot on high pressure, caramelize the onions in the oil. ~40 min [instant pot]
garlic + fresh ginger > purée: Purée together. [food processor]
chopped tomatoes > pulse: Pulse in a food processor until the chunks are smaller. [food processor]
caramelize + purée + pulse + dried red lentils + coconut milk + vegetable stock + dried chili flakes + cumin seeds + coriander seeds + mustard seeds + ground turmeric + garam masala + black pepper > combine: Add everything and stir; there should be enough liquid to fill the 8 quart pot to the brim.
combine > pressure: Cook on high pressure. Serve with rice, naan or poppadom. ~40 min
