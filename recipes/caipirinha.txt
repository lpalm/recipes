Caipirinha
portions 1
category Cocktail

produce:
1 1/2 limes, cut in 8 wedges, pith removed, washed
bar:
3 oz unaged cachaça
pantry:
2 tbsp white sugar
kitchen:
ice

limes + white sugar > muddle: Put the sugar and lime in a short glass and muddle on the smooth side. [short glass]
muddle + unaged cachaça + ice > mix: Add the cachaça and ice and mix with a spoon.
