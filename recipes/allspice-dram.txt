Allspice dram
portions 1

bar:
750 ml white rum
spices:
1 cup allspice berries, crushed
2 sticks cinnamon, broken
1 tsp nutmeg
pantry:
1/2 cup extra dark brown sugar
2 vanilla beans, split

white rum + allspice berries + cinnamon + nutmeg + extra dark brown sugar + vanilla beans > steep: Mix in an airtight jar and leave in a cool place for 3 weeks. [jar]
steep > strain: Strain into a bottle.
