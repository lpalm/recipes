Sour cream and onion popcorn seasoning
portions 1
category Snacks

pantry:
2 tbsp powdered milk
1/2 tsp citric acid
spices:
1 tsp onion powder
1/2 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp dried chives
1/2 tsp dried dill

powdered milk + citric acid + onion powder + garlic powder + dried chives + dried dill > mix: Mix and store airtight; shake over hot popcorn. [jar]
