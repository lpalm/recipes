Popcorn
portions 2
category Snacks

pantry:
1/3 cup popcorn kernels
1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
4 tbsp nutritional yeast, Marigold Engevita
spices:
1/4 tsp salt
kitchen:
1 tbsp water

salt + nutritional yeast > grind: Grind finely in a blender so it sticks to the popcorn. [blender]
popcorn kernels + water + toasted sesame oil + grind > pop: Mix the kernels with the water and oil, pop with the lid on until the popping slows, then toss with the yeast mix. [pot]
