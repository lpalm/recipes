Asian marinated chicken rice bowls
portions 2
category Non-vegan Mains

pantry:
2 cups jasmine rice, cooked
olive oil, for brushing
2 tbsp soy sauce
1 tbsp rice vinegar
2 drops sesame oil
1 tsp ginger paste
1 tsp sriracha
sesame seeds
4 tbsp mayo
1 tbsp sriracha for the mayo
meat:
chicken tenderloins
spices:
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp onion powder
produce:
1 avocado, mashed
lemon juice, a squeeze
green onions, sliced
lime juice, a squeeze, optional

jasmine rice + olive oil > crisp: Brush oil over the cooked rice and bake until crispy. ~22 min [oven 400 F]
chicken tenderloins + soy sauce + rice vinegar + sesame oil + ginger paste + sriracha + garlic powder + onion powder + sesame seeds > marinate: Combine and coat the chicken. [bowl]
marinate > air fry: Air fry at 360 F, then shred. ~12 min [air fryer 360 F]
crisp + air fry + mayo + sriracha for the mayo + lime juice + avocado + lemon juice + green onions > assemble: Mix the mayo, sriracha and lime into a spicy mayo, combine with the avocado, lemon juice, chicken and green onions, and pile onto the rice.
