Peanut protein ice cream
portions 1

dairy:
1/2 cup full fat Greek yogurt
1/2 cup 2% milk
pantry:
2 scoops peanut powder
2 tbsp peanut butter
2 packets Splenda

full fat Greek yogurt + 2% milk + peanut powder + peanut butter + Splenda > mix: Mix in the Creami pint until smooth. [Creami pint]
mix > freeze: Freeze flat. ~24 h
freeze > spin: Spin on Lite Ice Cream; re-spin if crumbly. [Ninja Creami]
