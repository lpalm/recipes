Blueberry oat muffins
portions 12
category Toddler

pantry:
1 cup rolled oats
1/2 cup unsweetened applesauce
1 tsp vanilla extract
1 1/2 tsp aluminum-free baking powder
produce:
1 ripe banana, medium
1 tsp lemon zest
3/4 cup blueberries, fresh or frozen
dairy:
3/4 cup whole milk Greek yogurt
1 large egg

rolled oats + ripe banana + whole milk Greek yogurt + unsweetened applesauce + large egg + vanilla extract + aluminum-free baking powder + lemon zest > blend: Add the oats first, then everything else, and blend until smooth. [blender]
blend + blueberries > bake: Stir in half the blueberries, pour into a greased or lined 12-cup muffin pan, top with the rest and bake until a toothpick comes out clean; cool at least 10 minutes in the pan. ~45 min [oven 350 F]
