Chicken piccata
portions 4
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
4 chicken breast cutlets, about 12 oz total
produce:
1 large lemon, zested
1/4 cup lemon juice, from the same lemon, more if needed
2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
pantry:
1/4 cup Dijon mustard, or spicy brown
2/3 cup all-purpose flour
1/4 cup olive oil, plus more as needed
1/2 cup dry white wine
1/4 cup low-sodium chicken stock
3 tbsp capers in brine, drained
dairy:
2 tbsp unsalted butter, cut in small cubes
spices:
fine salt
freshly cracked black pepper

large lemon + Dijon mustard > coating: Stir the zest into the mustard in a shallow bowl. [shallow bowl]
coating + chicken breast cutlets > coat: Coat the cutlets evenly with the mustard.
coat + all-purpose flour > dredge: Dredge in the flour until evenly coated.
olive oil + dredge > fry: Fry in shimmering oil over medium-high, in batches if needed, until deep golden, about 3 minutes a side and 160 F inside; keep warm on a plate. ~6 min [large skillet]
dry white wine + low-sodium chicken stock > deglaze: With the chicken out, add to the skillet, whisk up the browned bits and reduce by a quarter over medium-high. ~2 min
deglaze + lemon juice + unsalted butter > sauce: Whisk in the lemon juice, then the butter until smooth. ~2 min
sauce + capers in brine + fine salt + freshly cracked black pepper > season: Stir in the capers; taste and add lemon, salt and pepper as needed.
season + fresh parsley > herbs: Off the heat, stir in half the parsley.
fry + herbs > plate: Plate the cutlets, spoon 2 tbsp to 1/4 cup of sauce over each and sprinkle with the rest of the parsley.
