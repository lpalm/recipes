Chicken piccata
portions 4
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
4 chicken breast cutlets, about 12 oz total
produce:
1 large lemon, zested
1/4 cup lemon juice, from the same lemon, more if needed
2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
pantry:
1/4 cup Dijon mustard, or spicy brown
2/3 cup all-purpose flour
1/4 cup olive oil, plus more as needed
1/2 cup dry white wine
1/4 cup low-sodium chicken stock
3 tbsp capers in brine, drained
dairy:
2 tbsp unsalted butter, cut in small cubes
spices:
fine salt
freshly cracked black pepper

large lemon + Dijon mustard + chicken breast cutlets + all-purpose flour > dredge: Stir the zest into the mustard in a shallow bowl, coat the cutlets evenly, then dredge in the flour. [shallow bowl]
olive oil + dredge > fry: Fry in shimmering oil over medium-high, in batches if needed, until deep golden, about 3 minutes a side and 160 F inside; keep warm on a plate. ~6 min [large skillet]
dry white wine + low-sodium chicken stock + lemon juice + unsalted butter + capers in brine + fine salt + freshly cracked black pepper + fresh parsley > sauce: With the chicken out, add the wine and stock to the skillet, whisk up the browned bits and reduce by a quarter; whisk in the lemon juice, then the butter until smooth; stir in the capers, taste, add lemon, salt and pepper as needed, and off the heat half the parsley. ~5 min
fry + sauce > plate: Plate the cutlets, spoon 2 tbsp to 1/4 cup of sauce over each and sprinkle with the rest of the parsley.
