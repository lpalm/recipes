Everyday lentil soup
portions 4

produce:
2 cloves garlic, minced
2 small shallots, diced, or 1/2 white onion
4 large carrots, thinly sliced
4 stalks celery, thinly sliced
3 cups baby potatoes, roughly chopped bite-size
3 sprigs fresh rosemary or thyme
2 cups sturdy greens, kale or collards, chopped
fresh parsley, for garnish
pantry:
2 tbsp water, or oil
4 cups vegetable broth, plus more as needed
1 cup green or brown lentils, rinsed and drained
spices:
1/4 tsp sea salt
1/4 tsp black pepper

water + garlic + small shallots + large carrots + celery + sea salt + black pepper > sauté: Heat a large pot over medium and sauté with a little of the salt and pepper until slightly tender and golden; don't let the garlic burn. ~5 min [large pot]
sauté + baby potatoes > potatoes: Add, season a bit more and cook. ~2 min
potatoes + vegetable broth + fresh rosemary or thyme > broth: Add and bring to a rolling simmer over medium-high.
broth + green or brown lentils > simmer: Add the lentils, stir, and once simmering again reduce to low and simmer uncovered until lentils and potatoes are tender. ~18 min
simmer + sturdy greens > wilt: Add the greens, stir, cover and cook until wilted; adjust salt, pepper, broth or herbs. ~4 min
wilt + fresh parsley > serve: Garnish with parsley; keeps 5 days in the fridge.
