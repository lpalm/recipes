Everyday lentil soup
portions 4
category Other Vegan Mains

produce:
2 cloves garlic, minced
2 small shallots, diced, or 1/2 white onion
4 large carrots, thinly sliced
4 stalks celery, thinly sliced
3 cups baby potatoes, roughly chopped bite-size
3 sprigs fresh rosemary or thyme
2 cups sturdy greens, kale or collards, chopped
fresh parsley, for garnish
pantry:
2 tbsp water, or oil
4 cups vegetable broth, plus more as needed
1 cup green or brown lentils, rinsed and drained
spices:
1/4 tsp sea salt
1/4 tsp black pepper

water + garlic + small shallots + large carrots + celery + sea salt + black pepper + baby potatoes > sauté: Heat a large pot over medium and sauté the garlic, shallots, carrots and celery with a little salt and pepper until slightly tender and golden, about 5 minutes (don't let the garlic burn); add the potatoes, season a bit more and cook 2 minutes. ~7 min [large pot]
sauté + vegetable broth + fresh rosemary or thyme + green or brown lentils > simmer: Add the broth and herbs, bring to a rolling simmer over medium-high, add the lentils, stir, and once simmering again reduce to low and simmer uncovered until lentils and potatoes are tender. ~18 min
simmer + sturdy greens + fresh parsley > wilt: Add the greens, stir, cover and cook until wilted; adjust salt, pepper, broth or herbs, and garnish with parsley. ~4 min
