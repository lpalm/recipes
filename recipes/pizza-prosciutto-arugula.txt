Pizza with prosciutto and arugula
portions 1
category Pizza Making

bakery:
250 g Neapolitan pizza dough
pantry:
80 g tomato sauce
1 tsp olive oil
dairy:
80 g mozzarella
5 g Parmigiano Reggiano, shaved or grated
meat:
30 g prosciutto, up to {40 g}; {2 to 3} thin slices, chopped
produce:
10 g arugula, up to {15 g}; about {1 handful}

Neapolitan pizza dough + tomato sauce + mozzarella > bake: Make a dough ball for each portion. Stretch each, distribute the sauce and mozzarella, and bake in the Ooni at about {=80}% of its maximum. ~2 min [Ooni]
bake + prosciutto + arugula + Parmigiano Reggiano + olive oil > finish: Top with the prosciutto, arugula, Parmigiano and a drizzle of oil.
