Pizza with prosciutto and arugula
portions 1

bakery:
250 g Neapolitan pizza dough
pantry:
80 g tomato sauce
1 tsp olive oil
dairy:
80 g mozzarella
5 g Parmigiano Reggiano, shaved or grated
meat:
35 g prosciutto, 2 to 3 thin slices, chopped
produce:
12 g arugula, a small handful

Neapolitan pizza dough + tomato sauce + mozzarella > build: Stretch, sauce, mozzarella.
build > bake: Bake in the Ooni at about 80% of its maximum. ~2 min [Ooni]
bake + prosciutto + arugula + Parmigiano Reggiano + olive oil > finish: Top with the prosciutto, arugula, Parmigiano and a drizzle of oil.
