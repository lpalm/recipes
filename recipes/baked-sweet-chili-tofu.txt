Baked sweet chili tofu
portions 2
category Tofu Vegan Mains

pantry:
14 oz extra-firm tofu, pressed and cut in 1/2 inch cubes
1 tbsp low-sodium tamari
1 tbsp sesame seeds
2 tbsp sweet chili sauce
2 tbsp sweet chili sauce for the glaze
3 tbsp cornstarch

extra-firm tofu + low-sodium tamari + sesame seeds + sweet chili sauce + cornstarch > coat: In a large bowl drizzle the tofu with the tamari, sesame seeds and sweet chili sauce and fold gently until coated; sprinkle the cornstarch on top and fold again until evenly distributed. [bowl]
coat > bake: Spread on a lined baking sheet with space between the pieces; bake on the top rack 13 minutes, flip, and bake 12 to 14 more until golden and crispy. ~26 min [oven 425 F]
bake + sweet chili sauce for the glaze > glaze: Toss the baked tofu in a clean bowl with the remaining sweet chili sauce. Keeps 5 days.
