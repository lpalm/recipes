Vegetable fried rice
portions 4
category Sides

produce:
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
1 carrot, diced
1 red bell pepper, diced
4 green onions, sliced, white parts
green onion tops, the green parts, sliced
frozen:
1 cup frozen peas
dairy:
2 large eggs, lightly whisked
pantry:
2 tbsp cooking oil
1 tbsp cooking oil for the rice
3 cups cooked rice, cooled
3 tbsp soy sauce
1 tbsp toasted sesame oil

cooking oil + large eggs > scramble: Heat a large skillet over medium, swirl in the oil, add the eggs and scramble gently until cooked; move to a plate. [large skillet]
carrot + red bell pepper + green onions + frozen peas > vegetables: In the oil left in the skillet on medium-high, cook the carrots 2 minutes, add the pepper and the white parts of the onions for 1 minute, then the peas until heated through; move to a bowl. ~4 min
cooking oil for the rice + garlic + fresh ginger + cooked rice + soy sauce + toasted sesame oil > rice: Swirl in the remaining oil, add the garlic, ginger and rice and cook about 2 minutes until hot; pour over the soy sauce and sesame oil, stir until even and turn off the heat. ~2 min
rice + scramble + vegetables + green onion tops > combine: Return the eggs and vegetables, stir, and top with the green onion tops. Adjust soy or sesame oil to taste.
