Vegetable fried rice
portions 4

produce:
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
1 carrot, diced
1 red bell pepper, diced
4 green onions, sliced, white parts
green onion tops, the green parts, sliced
frozen:
1 cup frozen peas
dairy:
2 large eggs, lightly whisked
pantry:
2 tbsp cooking oil
1 tbsp cooking oil for the rice
3 cups cooked rice, cooled
3 tbsp soy sauce
1 tbsp toasted sesame oil

cooking oil + large eggs > scramble: Heat a large skillet over medium, swirl in the oil, add the eggs and scramble gently until cooked; move to a plate. [large skillet]
carrot > carrots: In the oil left in the skillet, on medium-high, cook the carrots. ~2 min
carrots + red bell pepper + green onions > peppers: Add the pepper and the white parts of the onions; cook 1 minute more.
peppers + frozen peas > vegetables: Add the peas and cook until heated through; move to a bowl.
cooking oil for the rice + garlic + fresh ginger + cooked rice > rice: Swirl in the remaining oil, add the garlic, ginger and rice and cook until hot. ~2 min
rice + soy sauce + toasted sesame oil > season: Pour over the rice and stir until even; turn off the heat.
season + scramble + vegetables + green onion tops > combine: Return the eggs and vegetables, stir, and top with the green onion tops. Adjust soy or sesame oil to taste.
