Skillet asparagus with lemon and Parmigiano
portions 2
category Sides

produce:
8 g garlic, thinly sliced
500 g asparagus, trimmed
15 g lemon juice
pantry:
25 g olive oil
dairy:
35 g Parmigiano Reggiano, finely grated
spices:
1 g black pepper

olive oil + garlic > sizzle: Heat a wide frying pan on medium, add the oil and garlic and cook 30 to 45 seconds until fragrant, without browning. [wide frying pan]
sizzle + asparagus > cook: Add the asparagus, raise to medium-high and cook uncovered, tossing now and then, until lightly browned. ~7 min
cook + lemon juice > reduce: Push the asparagus aside, pour the lemon juice onto the empty part of the pan and let it reduce by half, 30 to 60 seconds, then turn off the heat and mix.
reduce + black pepper + Parmigiano Reggiano > finish: Add the pepper and Parmigiano, toss and serve.
