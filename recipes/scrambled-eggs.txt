Scrambled eggs
portions 1
category Breakfast

dairy:
1 egg
1/2 tbsp butter
20 g Parmigiano Reggiano, grated
spices:
1 pinch salt and pepper

egg + salt and pepper > beat: Crack the egg into a measuring jug, add a tiny pinch of salt and pepper and beat well with a fork. [jug]
butter + beat + Parmigiano Reggiano > scramble: Melt the butter slowly in a frying pan over low heat until it bubbles, pour in the egg and stir slowly right to the edges until silky and a little underdone; off the heat fold in the Parmigiano. Serve with buttered toast. [frying pan]
