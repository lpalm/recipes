Scrambled eggs
portions 1

dairy:
1 egg
1/2 tbsp butter
20 g Parmigiano Reggiano, grated
spices:
1 pinch salt and pepper

egg + salt and pepper > beat: Crack the egg into a measuring jug, add a tiny pinch of salt and pepper and beat well with a fork. [jug]
butter > melt: Melt slowly in a frying pan over low heat until it starts to bubble. [frying pan]
melt + beat > scramble: Pour in the egg and stir slowly with a spatula, right to the edges, until silky, slightly runny and a little underdone, then take off the heat.
scramble + Parmigiano Reggiano > finish: Fold in the Parmigiano and serve with buttered toast.
