Tuscan lemon rosemary white bean soup
portions 4
category Other Vegan Mains

produce:
1/2 cup onion, diced
1/4 cup celery, diced
1/4 cup carrot, diced
3 cloves garlic, minced
3 sprigs rosemary
1/2 lemon, juiced
1 handful kale, torn
pantry:
neutral oil, as needed
4 cups vegetable broth
2 Parmesan rinds, optional
2 1/2 cups white beans, cooked and drained
spices:
1/2 tsp red chili flakes, up to 1
salt, to taste

neutral oil + onion + celery + carrot + garlic + red chili flakes + rosemary > sauté: Drizzle oil in a medium pot over medium-high and sauté the onion, celery and carrot until soft, about 5 minutes; turn to medium, add the garlic for a minute, then the chili flakes and rosemary to toast for one more. ~7 min [medium pot]
sauté + vegetable broth + Parmesan rinds + white beans + lemon + salt > simmer: Add the broth, scraping up the browned bits, then the rinds, beans and lemon juice; season and simmer. ~20 min
simmer + kale > blend: Remove the rosemary and rinds, blend with an immersion blender to the texture you like, then stir in the kale and let it wilt before serving. [immersion blender]
