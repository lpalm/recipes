Gumbo
portions 8
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
2 lb andouille sausage, cut in 1/2 inch slices
2 lb chicken thighs, cut in 1 to 2 inch pieces
pantry:
1 tbsp avocado oil
1/4 cup avocado oil for the roux
1/2 cup white whole wheat flour
8 cups chicken stock
4 tbsp tomato paste
1 tbsp cornstarch
1 cube chicken bouillon
cooked white rice, 1/2 cup per bowl
produce:
1 large onion, chopped
1 bell pepper, chopped
3 stalks celery, chopped
8 cloves garlic, chopped
spices:
1 tbsp Cajun seasoning
1 tbsp dried thyme
1 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
sea salt and black pepper, to taste

avocado oil + andouille sausage + chicken thighs > brown: Heat the oil on sauté and brown the sausage on both sides without the lid, about 5 minutes; move to a plate, leaving the drippings, and brown the chicken in them, about 6 minutes; add to the plate. ~11 min [pressure cooker]
avocado oil for the roux + white whole wheat flour + Cajun seasoning + dried thyme + smoked paprika > roux: Add to the pot and stir constantly until the roux is the color of peanut butter. ~15 min
roux + large onion + bell pepper + celery + garlic > soften: Add to the darkened roux and cook, stirring often, until soft. ~5 min
soften + brown + chicken stock + cornstarch + tomato paste + chicken bouillon > pressure: Return the meats, add the stock, cornstarch, tomato paste and bouillon, stir up anything stuck to the bottom, and pressure cook on manual 15 minutes, then natural release 15 minutes. ~30 min
pressure + sea salt and black pepper + cooked white rice > serve: Taste, season, and serve over 1/2 cup of rice per bowl.
