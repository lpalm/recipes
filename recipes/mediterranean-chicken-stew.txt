Mediterranean chicken stew
portions 6
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
produce:
1 large yellow onion, diced
4 cloves garlic, minced
2 medium carrots, sliced
1/2 lemon, zest and juice
2 handfuls baby spinach, optional
1/4 cup parsley, chopped, optional
1/2 cup green or Kalamata olives, sliced
pantry:
2 tbsp olive oil
1 can crushed tomatoes, 28 oz
1 can chickpeas, 15 oz, drained and rinsed
1 cup chicken broth
spices:
1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
1/2 tsp black pepper
1 tsp dried oregano
1 tsp ground cumin
1 tsp smoked paprika
1/2 tsp cinnamon
1 bay leaf

olive oil + boneless skinless chicken thighs + kosher salt + black pepper > sear: Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high, season the thighs and sear 3 to 4 minutes a side until lightly golden, not cooked through; move to a plate. ~7 min [dutch oven]
large yellow onion + medium carrots > soften: In the same pot, cook until softened. ~6 min
soften + garlic > fragrant: Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds.
fragrant + crushed tomatoes + chickpeas + chicken broth + dried oregano + ground cumin + smoked paprika + cinnamon + bay leaf > stew: Stir in, scraping the browned bits from the bottom.
stew + sear > simmer: Return the chicken and its juices, bring to a gentle simmer, partially cover and simmer until tender. ~28 min
simmer > shred: Take out the chicken, shred roughly with two forks and return it.
shred + green or Kalamata olives + lemon + baby spinach > finish: Add the olives, lemon zest and juice and spinach; simmer until the spinach wilts. Adjust salt and lemon, remove the bay leaf. Better the next day. ~3 min
finish + parsley > serve: Sprinkle with parsley; serve with couscous, orzo or bread.
