Mediterranean chicken stew
portions 6
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
produce:
1 large yellow onion, diced
4 cloves garlic, minced
2 medium carrots, sliced
1/2 lemon, zest and juice
2 handfuls baby spinach, optional
1/4 cup parsley, chopped, optional
1/2 cup green or Kalamata olives, sliced
pantry:
2 tbsp olive oil
1 can crushed tomatoes, 28 oz
1 can chickpeas, 15 oz, drained and rinsed
1 cup chicken broth
spices:
1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
1/2 tsp black pepper
1 tsp dried oregano
1 tsp ground cumin
1 tsp smoked paprika
1/2 tsp cinnamon
1 bay leaf

olive oil + boneless skinless chicken thighs + kosher salt + black pepper > sear: Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high, season the thighs and sear 3 to 4 minutes a side until lightly golden, not cooked through; move to a plate. ~7 min [dutch oven]
large yellow onion + medium carrots + garlic > soften: In the same pot cook the onion and carrots until softened, about 6 minutes, then the garlic for 30 seconds. ~6 min
soften + crushed tomatoes + chickpeas + chicken broth + dried oregano + ground cumin + smoked paprika + cinnamon + bay leaf + sear > simmer: Stir in, scraping the browned bits from the bottom, return the chicken and its juices, bring to a gentle simmer, partially cover and simmer until tender. ~28 min
simmer + green or Kalamata olives + lemon + baby spinach + parsley > finish: Take out the chicken, shred roughly with two forks and return it; add the olives, lemon zest and juice and spinach, simmer until the spinach wilts, adjust salt and lemon, remove the bay leaf and sprinkle with parsley. Better the next day; serve with couscous, orzo or bread. ~3 min
