Garlic chili tofu with sesame broccolini
portions 2

pantry:
340 g extra firm tofu, cut in 15 mm cubes
8 g sesame oil
4 g sesame oil for the broccolini
9 g toasted sesame seeds
14 g vegetable oil
15 g soy sauce
7 g rice vinegar
12 g sugar
produce:
10 g garlic, crushed
225 g broccolini
6 g garlic for the sauce, minced
spices:
3 g salt
1 g black pepper
5 g red pepper flakes
kitchen:
25 g water
40 g water for the sauce

extra firm tofu > press: Lay on paper towels and press firmly once with more towels for 10 to 15 seconds.
sesame oil + press > sear: Heat the oil in a wide nonstick pan over medium-high, add the tofu in one layer and cook 6 to 8 minutes without moving until golden underneath; flip and cook 3 to 4 more. Remove to a plate. ~11 min [wide nonstick pan]
sesame oil for the broccolini + garlic > sizzle: Add to the same pan and cook about 1 minute. ~1 min
sizzle + broccolini + water > steam: Add and cover 2 to 3 minutes (skip if frozen), then uncover and cook on medium until the water is gone. ~4 min
steam + salt + black pepper + toasted sesame seeds > season: Add and mix.
vegetable oil + garlic for the sauce + red pepper flakes + soy sauce + rice vinegar + sugar + water for the sauce > sauce: Mix in a bowl. [bowl]
season + sear + sauce > glaze: Return the tofu, pour in the sauce and cook, stirring, until glossy and slightly reduced. Serve at once. ~2 min
