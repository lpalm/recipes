Pizza Margherita
portions 1
category Pizza Making

bakery:
250 g Neapolitan pizza dough
pantry:
80 g tomato sauce
6 g extra virgin olive oil
produce:
5 leaves fresh basil
dairy:
80 g fresh mozzarella, fior di latte or buffalo, drained and torn in 1 inch pieces
10 g Parmigiano Reggiano

Neapolitan pizza dough + tomato sauce + fresh basil + fresh mozzarella > bake: Stretch the dough, spread the sauce, lay the basil, then the mozzarella, and bake in the Ooni at about 80% of its maximum. ~2 min [Ooni]
bake + extra virgin olive oil + Parmigiano Reggiano > finish: Drizzle with oil and grate the Parmigiano over.
