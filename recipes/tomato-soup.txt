Tomato soup
portions 8
category Sides

dairy:
4 tbsp unsalted butter
1/2 cup heavy whipping cream
1/3 cup Parmesan, freshly grated
produce:
2 yellow onions, finely chopped
3 cloves garlic, minced
1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
pantry:
56 oz crushed tomatoes, two 28 oz cans, preferably San Marzano
2 cups chicken stock
1 tbsp sugar
spices:
1/2 tsp black pepper

unsalted butter + yellow onions > soften: In a nonreactive pot over medium heat, sauté the onions in the butter until softened and golden. ~11 min [dutch oven]
soften + garlic > fragrant: Add the garlic and sauté 1 minute.
fragrant + crushed tomatoes + chicken stock + fresh basil + sugar + black pepper > simmer: Stir in, bring to a boil, then partially cover and simmer. ~10 min
simmer > blend: Blend with an immersion blender to the consistency you like. [immersion blender]
blend + heavy whipping cream + Parmesan > finish: Add the cream and Parmesan, return to a simmer, season with salt and pepper and turn off the heat. Serve with more Parmesan and basil.
