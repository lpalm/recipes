Arugula salad
portions 1
category Sides

produce:
40 g arugula
dairy:
10 g goat cheese, or 6 g shaved Parmesan
pantry:
11 g olive oil
8 g balsamic vinegar
10 g toasted pecans
spices:
0.6 g salt
0.15 g black pepper

arugula + olive oil + balsamic vinegar + salt + black pepper + goat cheese + toasted pecans > dress: Toss the leaves with the oil, vinegar, salt and pepper and top with the goat cheese and pecans. [bowl]
