Vermont farmhouse egg cream
portions 1
category Cocktail

dairy:
1 oz half and half
1 egg white
bar:
1 oz vanilla maple walnut liqueur, or dark maple syrup
1 oz bourbon
1 oz brandy
2 dashes Dutch's Colonial bitters
pantry:
2 oz seltzer
spices:
grated nutmeg

half and half + vanilla maple walnut liqueur + bourbon + brandy + egg white > dry shake: Dry shake without ice. [shaker]
dry shake + Dutch's Colonial bitters > shake: Add the bitters and ice and shake again.
shake + seltzer > build: Pour into a highball glass with ice and top with the seltzer. [highball glass]
build + grated nutmeg > garnish: Garnish with grated nutmeg.
