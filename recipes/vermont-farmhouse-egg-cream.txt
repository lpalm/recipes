Vermont farmhouse egg cream
portions 1
category Cocktail

dairy:
1 oz half and half
1 egg white
bar:
1 oz vanilla maple walnut liqueur, or dark maple syrup
1 oz bourbon
1 oz brandy
2 dashes Dutch's Colonial bitters
pantry:
2 oz seltzer
spices:
grated nutmeg

half and half + vanilla maple walnut liqueur + bourbon + brandy + egg white + Dutch's Colonial bitters + seltzer + grated nutmeg > shake: Dry shake without ice, add the bitters and ice and shake again; pour into a highball glass with ice, top with the seltzer and grate nutmeg over. [shaker]
