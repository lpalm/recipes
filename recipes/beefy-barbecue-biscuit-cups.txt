Beefy barbecue biscuit cups
portions 4
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
1 lb ground beef
produce:
1/3 cup red onion, finely diced, optional
pantry:
1/3 cup barbecue sauce
1 tube flaky refrigerated biscuits, 8 count
frozen:
1/2 cup frozen corn
dairy:
1 cup mild Cheddar, shredded

ground beef + red onion + barbecue sauce + frozen corn > filling: Cook the beef and onion in a skillet over medium until no longer pink, drain the grease, then mix in the barbecue sauce and corn. [skillet]
flaky refrigerated biscuits > cups: Split each biscuit in half along the layers, flatten each half to a 4 to 5 inch circle and press into sprayed muffin cups. [muffin tin]
cups + filling > bake: Spoon 2 to 3 tbsp of beef into each cup and bake. ~10 min [oven 400 F]
bake + mild Cheddar > melt: Sprinkle with cheese and bake until bubbly. To freeze instead, cool without cheese, freeze, and reheat at 350 F for 10 to 15 minutes before adding the cheese. ~4 min
