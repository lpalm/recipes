Instant Pot salsa chicken
portions 6
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
2 lb chicken breasts, or thighs
produce:
1/2 yellow onion, diced
2 cloves garlic, minced
pantry:
1 1/2 cups salsa, your favorite
spices:
2 tsp chili powder
1 tsp smoked paprika
1 tsp cumin
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp onion powder
1/2 tsp Mexican oregano
salt

chili powder + smoked paprika + cumin + garlic powder + onion powder + Mexican oregano + yellow onion + garlic > taco seasoning: Mix together in a small bowl. [bowl]
chicken breasts + salt + taco seasoning + salsa > coat: Chicken in the instant pot, salt it, rub in the seasoning, add the salsa and mix so all the chicken is coated. [instant pot]
coat > pressure: Valve to sealing; cook on high pressure, release, cool slightly and shred on a cutting board for tacos, bowls or on its own. ~13 min
