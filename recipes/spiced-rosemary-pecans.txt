Spiced rosemary pecans
portions 8
category Snacks

pantry:
1 lb unsalted pecans, halves or whole
3 tbsp light brown sugar
dairy:
1/2 cup butter, melted
produce:
3 tbsp fresh rosemary, coarsely chopped
spices:
2 tsp cayenne pepper
1 tbsp salt

unsalted pecans + butter + fresh rosemary + light brown sugar + cayenne pepper + salt > bake: Toss everything in a bowl, spread on a parchment-lined sheet tray and bake. ~10 min [oven 350 F]
