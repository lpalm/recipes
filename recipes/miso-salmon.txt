Miso salmon
portions 2

fish:
2 salmon fillets, 6 oz each
pantry:
2 tbsp miso paste
1 tbsp soy sauce
1/2 tbsp tamari
1 tbsp mirin
1 tbsp rice vinegar
1 tbsp brown sugar
1/2 tsp sesame oil
sesame seeds, for garnish
spices:
salt and pepper

miso paste + soy sauce + tamari + mirin + brown sugar + rice vinegar + sesame oil > marinade: Whisk until smooth; reserve 1 tbsp for the glaze. [bowl]
salmon fillets + salt and pepper > season: Salt and pepper the salmon.
season + marinade > marinate: Add the salmon and marinate 1 to 3 hours. ~1 h
marinate > bake: Discard the marinade, put the salmon in a large skillet and bake until almost done and opaque. ~13 min [oven 375 F]
bake > broil: Brush with the reserved glaze and broil on high until 125 to 130 F in the centre. ~2 min
broil + sesame seeds > garnish: Sprinkle with sesame seeds.
