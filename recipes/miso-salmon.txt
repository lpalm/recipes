Miso salmon
portions 2
category Non-vegan Mains

fish:
2 salmon fillets, 6 oz each
pantry:
2 tbsp miso paste
1 tbsp soy sauce
1/2 tbsp tamari
1 tbsp mirin
1 tbsp rice vinegar
1 tbsp brown sugar
1/2 tsp sesame oil
sesame seeds, for garnish
spices:
salt and pepper

miso paste + soy sauce + tamari + mirin + brown sugar + rice vinegar + sesame oil + salmon fillets + salt and pepper > marinate: Whisk the marinade until smooth and reserve 1 tbsp for the glaze; salt and pepper the salmon and marinate it 1 to 3 hours. ~1 h [bowl]
marinate > bake: Discard the marinade, put the salmon in a large skillet and bake until almost done and opaque. ~13 min [oven 375 F]
bake + sesame seeds > broil: Brush with the reserved glaze and broil on high until 125 to 130 F in the centre; sprinkle with sesame seeds. ~2 min
