Mushroom risotto
portions 4
category Non-vegan Mains

produce:
50 g dried porcini mushrooms
1 onion, finely chopped, or 1/3 cup shallots
2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
250 g chestnut mushrooms, chopped
1 handful parsley, chopped, or chives
pantry:
1 cube vegetable stock
2 tbsp olive oil
300 g risotto rice, arborio
175 ml white wine
dairy:
25 g butter
50 g Parmesan, or Grana Padano, freshly grated
spices:
salt and pepper
kitchen:
1 l boiling water

dried porcini mushrooms + boiling water > soak: Pour the boiling water over the porcini in a large bowl and soak 20 minutes; drain into a bowl, leaving the last gritty spoonfuls behind, and squeeze the mushrooms. ~20 min [bowl]
soak + vegetable stock > stock: Crumble the stock cube into the mushroom liquid; keep the squeezed porcini for the pan.
olive oil + onion + garlic > soften: Heat the oil in a deep frying pan over medium and fry until soft. ~5 min [deep frying pan]
soften + chestnut mushrooms + salt and pepper > mushrooms: Stir in the chestnut mushrooms and the soaked porcini, season, and cook until the fresh mushrooms soften. ~8 min
mushrooms + risotto rice > toast: Tip in the rice and cook 1 minute. ~1 min
toast + white wine > wine: Pour in the wine and let it bubble to nothing.
wine + stock > simmer: Over medium heat add a quarter of the stock at a time, stirring often and letting each be absorbed before the next, until the rice is creamy, plump and tender; a splash of water if undercooked. ~25 min
simmer + butter + Parmesan + parsley > finish: Off the heat add the butter, half the cheese and half the parsley; cover and rest a few minutes, stir, and serve topped with the rest of the cheese and parsley.
