Milk punch
portions 1
category Cocktail

dairy:
3 oz milk
bar:
2 oz bourbon, Four Roses yellow label
pantry:
1 oz simple syrup
1 tsp vanilla extract
spices:
grated nutmeg

milk + bourbon + simple syrup + vanilla extract > shake: Shake with ice and strain into a glass. [shaker]
shake + grated nutmeg > garnish: Grate nutmeg over the top.
