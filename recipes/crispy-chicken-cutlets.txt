Crispy chicken cutlets
portions 4
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
1 1/2 lb chicken cutlets
pantry:
1 cup seasoned breadcrumbs
1/4 cup yellow cornmeal
olive oil spray
dairy:
1/4 cup Parmesan, freshly grated
2 eggs
spices:
kosher salt
black pepper

seasoned breadcrumbs + yellow cornmeal + Parmesan > crumbs: Stir together in a shallow bowl. [shallow bowl]
eggs > beat: Beat in another shallow bowl. [shallow bowl]
chicken cutlets + kosher salt + black pepper > season: Pat dry and season.
season + beat + crumbs > bread: Dip each cutlet in the egg, then the crumbs, and arrange on the sprayed air fry trays; work in batches if needed. [air fry trays]
bread + olive oil spray > air fry: Spray with oil and air fry at 400 F on the highest and lowest racks, turning the cutlets and swapping the trays after 6 minutes, until golden, crisp and 165 F inside. ~12 min [air fryer 400 F]
