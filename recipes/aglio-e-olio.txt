Spaghetti aglio e olio
portions 2

produce:
3 cloves garlic, sliced thin
1 handful parsley, chopped
pantry:
200 g spaghetti
60 ml olive oil
spices:
1 tbsp salt
1/2 tsp chili flakes
kitchen:
2 l water

water + salt > boil: Bring to a rolling boil in a large pot. ~8 min [large pot]
boil + spaghetti > cook: Cook 1 min short of al dente. Keep a cup of the water before draining. ~9 min
olive oil + garlic + chili flakes > sizzle: Warm over low heat until the garlic is golden, not brown. ~4 min [wide pan]
cook + sizzle + parsley > toss: Toss in the pan with a splash of pasta water until glossy. Serve at once.
