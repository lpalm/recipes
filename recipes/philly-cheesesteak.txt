Philly cheesesteak
portions 2

meat:
350 g thin-sliced beef, shaved ribeye
bakery:
2 hoagie rolls
produce:
180 g yellow onion, thin-sliced
90 g green bell pepper, sliced, optional
100 g mushrooms, sliced, optional
dairy:
80 g low-moisture mozzarella, thin-sliced or shredded
20 g Gruyère, finely grated, optional
pantry:
10 g neutral oil, or beef fat
5 g neutral oil for the beef
spices:
1 g salt
3 g salt for the beef
1.5 g black pepper
kitchen:
30 ml water

hoagie rolls > warm: Wrap each roll loosely in foil and air fry at 325 F; keep wrapped until assembly. ~4 min [air fryer 325 F]
neutral oil + yellow onion + salt > onions: Pan on medium: oil, onions and salt; cook, stirring now and then, until soft and lightly browned. ~11 min [large pan]
onions + green bell pepper + mushrooms > vegetables: Cook the peppers and mushrooms in the same pan after the onions, 5 to 8 minutes, then move everything to a bowl and leave the pan on. ~6 min
neutral oil for the beef + thin-sliced beef > sear: Pan on high, very hot; add the oil only if dry, spread the beef thin (2 batches in a small pan) and sear 30 to 45 seconds without touching. If the beef is not shaved, freeze it 15 minutes first and slice thin.
sear + salt for the beef + black pepper > chop: Season, chop and flip, and cook another 30 to 45 seconds; stop while juicy, 60 to 90 seconds total.
chop + vegetables > combine: Return the onions and vegetables, mix 10 to 15 seconds and split into 2 roll-sized piles.
combine + low-moisture mozzarella + Gruyère + water > melt: Mozzarella over each pile, Gruyère on top, pour the water into the pan edge away from the meat, cover at once and steam 30 to 60 seconds until melted; uncover and fold each pile once or twice.
melt + warm > assemble: Open the rolls without separating them, scoop each pile in and serve at once.
