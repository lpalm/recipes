Philly cheesesteak
portions 2
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
350 g thin-sliced beef, shaved ribeye
bakery:
2 hoagie rolls
produce:
180 g yellow onion, thin-sliced
90 g green bell pepper, sliced, optional
100 g mushrooms, sliced, optional
dairy:
80 g low-moisture mozzarella, thin-sliced or shredded
20 g Gruyère, finely grated, optional
pantry:
10 g neutral oil, or beef fat
5 g neutral oil for the beef
spices:
1 g salt
3 g salt for the beef
1.5 g black pepper
kitchen:
30 ml water

hoagie rolls > warm: Wrap each roll loosely in foil and air fry at 325 F; keep wrapped until assembly. ~4 min [air fryer 325 F]
neutral oil + yellow onion + salt + green bell pepper + mushrooms > vegetables: Pan on medium: oil, onions and salt; cook, stirring now and then, until soft and lightly browned, 10 to 12 minutes; then the peppers and mushrooms in the same pan 5 to 8 minutes; move everything to a bowl and leave the pan on. ~17 min [large pan]
neutral oil for the beef + thin-sliced beef + salt for the beef + black pepper > sear: Pan on high, very hot; add the oil only if dry, spread the beef thin (2 batches in a small pan) and sear 30 to 45 seconds without touching; season, chop and flip, and cook another 30 to 45 seconds; stop while juicy. If the beef is not shaved, freeze it 15 minutes first and slice thin.
sear + vegetables + low-moisture mozzarella + Gruyère + water > melt: Return the onions and vegetables, mix 10 to 15 seconds and split into 2 roll-sized piles; mozzarella over each, Gruyère on top, pour the water into the pan edge away from the meat, cover at once and steam 30 to 60 seconds until melted; uncover and fold each pile once or twice.
melt + warm > assemble: Open the rolls without separating them, scoop each pile in and serve at once.
