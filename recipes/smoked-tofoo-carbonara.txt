Smoked Tofoo carbonara
portions 2
category Tofu Vegan Mains

pantry:
1 pack smoked Tofoo, diced
1 tbsp olive oil
200 g spaghetti
produce:
3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
4 shallots, finely chopped
8 chestnut mushrooms, sliced
fresh flat leaf parsley, for garnish
bar:
150 ml white wine
dairy:
150 ml soya single cream
vegan Parmesan, for garnish
spices:
salt and pepper
kitchen:
salted water

olive oil + smoked Tofoo + salt and pepper > fry: Heat a frying pan with the oil, add the Tofoo, season, and fry until lightly golden. [frying pan]
fry + shallots + garlic > soften: Add and cook until softened, not too long.
salted water + spaghetti > boil: Boil the pasta per the packet, about 10 minutes; start it around now. ~10 min [pot]
soften + chestnut mushrooms > mushrooms: Add and stir; cook a couple of minutes. ~2 min
mushrooms + white wine > reduce: Add the wine and cook until reduced. ~5 min
reduce + soya single cream > thicken: Add the cream, drop to medium-low and cook to thicken the sauce, not too much; season. ~5 min
thicken + boil > toss: Add the drained pasta and mix well.
toss + vegan Parmesan + fresh flat leaf parsley > serve: Serve with a grating of Parmesan and the parsley.
