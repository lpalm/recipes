Smoked Tofoo carbonara
portions 2
category Tofu Vegan Mains

pantry:
1 pack smoked Tofoo, diced
1 tbsp olive oil
200 g spaghetti
produce:
3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
4 shallots, finely chopped
8 chestnut mushrooms, sliced
fresh flat leaf parsley, for garnish
bar:
150 ml white wine
dairy:
150 ml soya single cream
vegan Parmesan, for garnish
spices:
salt and pepper
kitchen:
salted water

olive oil + smoked Tofoo + salt and pepper + shallots + garlic > fry: Heat a frying pan with the oil, add the Tofoo, season, and fry until lightly golden; add the shallots and garlic and cook until softened, not too long. [frying pan]
salted water + spaghetti > boil: Boil the pasta per the packet, about 10 minutes; start it around now. ~10 min [pot]
fry + chestnut mushrooms + white wine > reduce: Add the mushrooms, stir and cook a couple of minutes; add the wine and cook until reduced. ~7 min
reduce + soya single cream > thicken: Add the cream, drop to medium-low and cook to thicken the sauce, not too much; season. ~5 min
thicken + boil + vegan Parmesan + fresh flat leaf parsley > toss: Add the drained pasta, mix well and serve with a grating of Parmesan and the parsley.
