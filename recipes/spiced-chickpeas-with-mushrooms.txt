Spiced chickpeas with mushrooms and garlic
portions 2

pantry:
2 1/2 cups cooked chickpeas, one 19 oz can
1 tbsp olive oil
produce:
3 cloves garlic, minced
1/2 medium white onion, diced
2 cups white mushrooms, sliced
spices:
1 1/2 tsp paprika
1/2 tsp onion powder
1/2 tsp oregano
1/2 tsp black pepper
1/2 tsp sea salt
1/2 tsp chili powder
1/4 tsp thyme

olive oil + medium white onion + garlic + white mushrooms > brown: Heat the oil in a pan over medium, or use a splash of water or broth, and cook, stirring every couple of minutes, until they start to brown. ~7 min [pan]
brown + paprika + onion powder + oregano + black pepper + sea salt + chili powder + thyme > spice: Add the spices and cook a few minutes more, stirring often. ~3 min
spice + cooked chickpeas > finish: Add the chickpeas and cook, stirring, until heated through and browned. A handful of spinach at the end is good. ~8 min
