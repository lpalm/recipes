Rigatoni bolognese
portions 8
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
2 lb ground beef
produce:
300 g onion, minced
240 g mushrooms, minced
20 g garlic, minced
dairy:
40 g butter
100 g heavy cream, or 180 g half-and-half
pantry:
20 g high-heat oil
50 g tomato paste
120 g white wine, optional
1300 g Rao's roasted garlic sauce, 2 small jars
12 g Worcestershire sauce
10 g beef bouillon powder
590 g mezze rigatoni
spices:
0.8 g thyme
1 g black pepper
0.4 g chili flakes
kitchen:
salted water for the pasta

butter + garlic > gold: Pan 1 on medium: butter, then the garlic for 90 seconds until light gold. [frying pan]
gold + onion + mushrooms > vegetables: Add the onion and mushrooms and cook until the onion is very soft and the mushrooms are dry and well browned. ~30 min
high-heat oil + ground beef > brown: Pan 2 on high while the vegetables cook: oil, then beef; brown hard. ~7 min [frying pan]
brown + tomato paste > paste: Add the tomato paste and cook until deep brick red. ~3 min
paste + white wine > deglaze: Add the wine and reduce. ~2 min
deglaze + vegetables + Rao's roasted garlic sauce + Worcestershire sauce + beef bouillon powder + thyme + black pepper + chili flakes > combine: Add the vegetables to the beef pan with the sauce and seasonings.
combine > simmer: Simmer uncovered on low. ~28 min
simmer + heavy cream > cream: Add the cream for the final 5 minutes. ~5 min
salted water for the pasta + mezze rigatoni > boil: Boil the pasta during the final 14 minutes of the simmer. ~14 min [pasta pot]
cream + boil > toss: Toss the pasta with the sauce.
