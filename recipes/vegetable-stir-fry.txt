Vegetable stir fry
portions 2

frozen:
200 g stir fry vegetables
pantry:
1 tbsp toasted sesame oil for the pan
40 ml soy sauce, less sodium
1 tsp toasted sesame oil
2 tbsp rice vinegar
1 tbsp honey
1/2 cube vegetable stock
2 tbsp cornstarch
produce:
4 cloves garlic, sliced thin
spices:
ginger, powder or fresh
kitchen:
125 ml water

toasted sesame oil for the pan + stir fry vegetables + garlic > fry: Heat the oil in a thick frying pan until hot and fry the vegetables and garlic. ~4 min [frying pan]
soy sauce + water + toasted sesame oil + rice vinegar + honey + ginger + vegetable stock + cornstarch > sauce: Blend everything. [blender]
fry + sauce > glaze: Pour the sauce over the vegetables and cook 1 minute. ~1 min
