Quesadilla spice mix
portions 1
category Spice mixes

spices:
2 tbsp paprika
2 tbsp cumin powder
2 tsp onion powder
1 tsp dried oregano
1 tbsp Knorr chicken bouillon
1/4 tsp cayenne pepper

paprika + cumin powder + onion powder + dried oregano + Knorr chicken bouillon + cayenne pepper > mix: Mix and store airtight. Use 1 tbsp for 4 small quesadillas. [jar]
