Hot buttered rum
portions 1

dairy:
1 tbsp slightly salted butter
pantry:
2 tbsp brown sugar
1/4 tsp vanilla extract
spices:
0.5 g ground nutmeg
0.5 g ground cinnamon
0.25 g ground cloves
bar:
3 oz dark rum
kitchen:
4 oz boiling water

slightly salted butter + brown sugar + ground nutmeg + ground cinnamon + ground cloves + vanilla extract + boiling water > melt: Mix in a heat-resistant cup until the butter is liquid. [mug]
melt + dark rum > finish: Add the rum.
