Biscuits and gravy
portions 4
category Breakfast

meat:
1 lb hot breakfast sausage
1 lb sage breakfast sausage
dairy:
6 cups milk
6 tbsp frozen butter
2/3 cup milk for the biscuits
4 eggs
2 tbsp butter for the eggs
pantry:
4 tbsp cornstarch
2 cups flour
1 tbsp baking powder
1 tbsp granulated sugar
spices:
1/2 tsp salt
1/4 tsp black pepper
1 tsp salt for the biscuits
1/4 tsp salt for the eggs
1/4 tsp black pepper for the eggs

milk + cornstarch + salt + black pepper > slurry: Mix the cornstarch with a little cold milk until smooth, then add the rest. [large pot]
slurry > thicken: Heat, scraping the bottom every few minutes as it caramelizes, until thickened. ~15 min
hot breakfast sausage + sage breakfast sausage > brown: Break the sausage into tiny bits before it browns, brown it, then pour off some of the fat. ~10 min [pan]
thicken + brown > gravy: Add the sausage to the gravy and lower to a simmer.
flour + baking powder + granulated sugar + salt for the biscuits > dry mix: Mix the dry ingredients. [bowl]
dry mix + frozen butter > cut in: Cut the butter into tiny pieces into the flour.
cut in + milk for the biscuits > dough: Add the milk and mix without overworking, then knead gently to combine. Fold in half, turn 90 degrees and repeat 5 or 6 times.
dough > shape: Press out to 3/4 inch thick by hand, not with a rolling pin, and cut circles.
shape > bake: Bake until risen and golden. ~13 min [oven 450 F]
eggs + butter for the eggs + salt for the eggs + black pepper for the eggs > scramble: Scramble the eggs in the butter while the gravy simmers. [pan]
gravy + bake + scramble > serve: Split the biscuits, spoon the gravy over and serve with the eggs.
