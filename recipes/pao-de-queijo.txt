Pão de queijo
portions 4

pantry:
250 g Amafil pão de queijo mix
dairy:
2 eggs
100 g mozzarella, part moisture and full fat, grated
25 g Parmesan, grated
kitchen:
125 g water

Amafil pão de queijo mix + eggs + water + mozzarella + Parmesan > dough: Mix into a dough and roll into balls. [bowl]
dough > bake: Bake until puffed and golden. ~26 min [oven 400 F]
