Garlic lemon butter salmon
portions 2

fish:
2 skinless salmon fillets, 6 oz each, about 1 inch thick
produce:
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
1 tbsp fresh parsley, minced
pantry:
2 tsp olive oil
1/4 cup low-sodium chicken broth, or 1/5 cube in 1/4 cup water
1 tsp honey
dairy:
1 tsp unsalted butter
1 tbsp unsalted butter for the sauce
spices:
salt and freshly ground black pepper

unsalted butter + garlic > sauté: Melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium and sauté the garlic until lightly golden. ~2 min [small saucepan]
sauté + low-sodium chicken broth + fresh lemon juice > reduce: Pour in and simmer until reduced by half, to about 3 tbsp. ~3 min
reduce + unsalted butter for the sauce + honey > sauce: Whisk in the butter and honey and set aside.
skinless salmon fillets + salt and freshly ground black pepper > season: Rest the salmon 10 minutes at room temperature, dab dry and season both sides.
olive oil + season > sear: Heat the oil in a heavy 12 inch non-stick skillet over medium-high until shimmering; cook about 4 minutes until golden underneath, flip and cook 2 to 3 minutes more until cooked through. ~7 min [non-stick skillet]
sear + sauce + fresh parsley > plate: Spoon the sauce over the salmon and sprinkle with parsley.
