Garlic lemon butter salmon
portions 2
category Non-vegan Mains

fish:
2 skinless salmon fillets, 6 oz each, about 1 inch thick
produce:
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
1 tbsp fresh parsley, minced
pantry:
2 tsp olive oil
1/4 cup low-sodium chicken broth, or 1/5 cube in 1/4 cup water
1 tsp honey
dairy:
1 tsp unsalted butter
1 tbsp unsalted butter for the sauce
spices:
salt and freshly ground black pepper

unsalted butter + garlic + low-sodium chicken broth + fresh lemon juice + unsalted butter for the sauce + honey > sauce: Melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium, sauté the garlic until lightly golden, pour in the broth and lemon juice and simmer until reduced by half, to about 3 tbsp; whisk in the butter and honey and set aside. ~5 min [small saucepan]
skinless salmon fillets + salt and freshly ground black pepper + olive oil > sear: Rest the salmon 10 minutes at room temperature, dab dry and season both sides; heat the oil in a heavy 12 inch non-stick skillet over medium-high until shimmering, cook about 4 minutes until golden underneath, flip and cook 2 to 3 minutes more until cooked through. ~7 min [non-stick skillet]
sear + sauce + fresh parsley > plate: Spoon the sauce over the salmon and sprinkle with parsley.
