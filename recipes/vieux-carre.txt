Vieux Carré
portions 1

bar:
3/4 oz rye whiskey
3/4 oz cognac
1/2 oz Punt e Mes, or sweet vermouth
1/2 oz Bénédictine
1 dash Angostura bitters
kitchen:
1 ball ice, large

rye whiskey + cognac + Punt e Mes + Bénédictine + Angostura bitters + ice > build: Build in a short glass over the large ball of ice and stir. [short glass]
