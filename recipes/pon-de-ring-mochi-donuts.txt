Pon de ring baked mochi donuts
portions 9

pantry:
30 g sugar
25 g vegetable oil
20 g plain flour
90 g tapioca starch
dairy:
100 g milk
1 egg, beaten

milk + sugar + vegetable oil > heat: Heat in a saucepan until steaming. [saucepan]
heat + plain flour > paste: Add the flour and mix until smooth.
paste + tapioca starch > dough: Off the heat, add the tapioca starch and mix until a smooth ball forms.
dough + egg > batter: Move to a bowl and add the beaten egg slowly, stopping when the batter hangs from the spatula in a V shape.
batter > pipe: Pipe rings of small circles that just touch onto baking paper, using a round nozzle. [piping bag]
pipe > bake: Bake until lightly golden. ~13 min [oven 350 F]
