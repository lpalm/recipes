Pon de ring baked mochi donuts
portions 9
category Dessert

pantry:
30 g sugar
25 g vegetable oil
20 g plain flour
90 g tapioca starch
dairy:
100 g milk
1 egg, beaten

milk + sugar + vegetable oil + plain flour > paste: Heat the milk, sugar and oil in a saucepan until steaming, add the flour and mix until smooth. [saucepan]
paste + tapioca starch + egg > batter: Off the heat mix in the tapioca starch until a smooth ball forms; move to a bowl and add the beaten egg slowly, stopping when the batter hangs from the spatula in a V shape.
batter > bake: Pipe rings of small circles that just touch onto baking paper with a round nozzle and bake until lightly golden. ~13 min [oven 350 F] [piping bag]
