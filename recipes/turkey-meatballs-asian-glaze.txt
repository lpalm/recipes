Turkey meatballs with Asian glaze
portions 6
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
2 lb ground turkey breast
dairy:
2 eggs
pantry:
1 cup panko breadcrumbs, plain
cooking spray
1/2 cup hoisin sauce
1/4 cup reduced sodium soy sauce
1/4 cup rice wine vinegar
1 tbsp cornstarch
sesame seeds, for garnish
produce:
4 cloves garlic, minced
2 scallions, chopped
2 cloves garlic for the glaze, minced
1/2 tsp ginger, fresh or paste
scallions for garnish, chopped
spices:
1/4 tsp ground ginger
1 tsp kosher salt
fresh cracked pepper
kitchen:
3/4 cup water

ground turkey breast + eggs + panko breadcrumbs + garlic + scallions + ground ginger + kosher salt + fresh cracked pepper > mix: Combine in a large mixing bowl. [mixing bowl]
mix > form: Form 24 equal meatballs, about 1.5 oz each.
form + cooking spray > bake: Spray a mini muffin tin liberally, or use a baking sheet, one meatball per cup, and bake. ~15 min [oven 375 F] [mini muffin tin]
hoisin sauce + reduced sodium soy sauce + rice wine vinegar + cornstarch + water + garlic for the glaze + ginger > glaze: Whisk together in a saucepan and cook over medium, stirring often, until thickened. ~10 min [saucepan]
glaze + bake > coat: Add the meatballs to the sauce and cook a minute or two. ~2 min
coat + sesame seeds + scallions for garnish > garnish: Garnish.
