Turkey meatballs with Asian glaze
portions 12
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
4 lb ground turkey breast
dairy:
4 eggs
pantry:
2 cups panko breadcrumbs, plain
cooking spray
1 1/2 cups hoisin sauce
3/4 cup reduced sodium soy sauce
3/4 cup rice wine vinegar
3 tbsp cornstarch
sesame seeds, for garnish
produce:
8 cloves garlic, minced
4 scallions, chopped
6 cloves garlic for the glaze, minced
1 1/2 tsp ginger, fresh or paste
scallions for garnish, chopped
spices:
1/2 tsp ground ginger
2 tsp kosher salt
fresh cracked pepper
kitchen:
2 1/4 cups water

ground turkey breast + eggs + panko breadcrumbs + garlic + scallions + ground ginger + kosher salt + fresh cracked pepper > form: Combine in a large mixing bowl and form 4 equal meatballs per portion, about 1.5 oz each. [mixing bowl]
form + cooking spray > bake: Spray a mini muffin tin liberally, or use a baking sheet, one meatball per cup, and bake. ~15 min [oven 375 F] [mini muffin tin]
hoisin sauce + reduced sodium soy sauce + rice wine vinegar + cornstarch + water + garlic for the glaze + ginger > glaze: Whisk together in a saucepan and cook over medium, stirring often, until thickened. ~10 min [saucepan]
glaze + bake + sesame seeds + scallions for garnish > coat: Add the meatballs to the sauce, cook a minute or two, and garnish with sesame seeds and scallions. ~2 min
