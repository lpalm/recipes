Crispy sweet and sour tofu
portions 2
category Tofu Vegan Mains

pantry:
16 oz firm tofu, pressed, broken in 1 inch cubes
1/2 cup cornstarch
olive oil spray
oil for the pan
1/2 cup sugar
1/4 cup ketchup
1/4 cup vinegar, rice or white
1 tbsp soy sauce
1/2 tbsp sesame seeds, for garnish
produce:
1 medium bell pepper, chopped
1/2 cup pineapple, chopped
1/2 medium red onion, chopped
scallion, chopped, for garnish
spices:
1 tsp garlic powder
kitchen:
1/4 cup water

firm tofu + cornstarch + olive oil spray > air fry: Shake the tofu with the cornstarch in a zip bag, spread on the air fryer sheet, spray with oil, and cook at 375 F until crispy, shaking halfway and adding oil to dry spots. ~20 min [air fryer 375 F]
oil for the pan + medium red onion + pineapple + medium bell pepper > sauté: In a pan over medium-high with a little oil, sauté until the onion is translucent and the pineapple slightly browned. ~5 min [pan]
sauté + sugar + ketchup + vinegar + water + soy sauce + garlic powder > simmer: Whisk the sauce together, pour it over the vegetables, bring to a slight boil, then simmer until thickened. ~5 min
simmer + air fry + sesame seeds + scallion > combine: Add the crisp tofu and stir; adjust sugar or soy sauce; off the heat top with sesame seeds and scallion.
