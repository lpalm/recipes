Instant Pot pulled pork
portions 24
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
10 lb boneless skinless Boston butt, before trimming
pantry:
520 g unsalted chicken stock
18 g Worcestershire sauce
1 g hickory liquid smoke
16 g apple cider vinegar
spices:
33 g kosher salt
15 g smoked paprika
13 g garlic powder
11 g onion powder
6 g ground black pepper
1 g cayenne

boneless skinless Boston butt > cut pork: Trim the outer fat to at most {=6 mm} and cut the thawed pork into {=5 cm} chunks.
cut pork + kosher salt + smoked paprika + garlic powder + onion powder + ground black pepper + cayenne > season: Toss the pork with the dry spices.
season + unsalted chicken stock + Worcestershire sauce > pressure cook: Add the stock, Worcestershire sauce, and pork to the pot. Cook on High Pressure for {=45} minutes. ~45 min [8-quart Instant Pot]
pressure cook > release: Let the pressure release naturally. If the pork doesn’t shred easily, cook on High Pressure for {=10} more minutes, then release naturally again.
release > save juices: Remove the pork. Skim the fat from the cooking liquid and reserve {154 g} of the juices.
save juices + hickory liquid smoke + apple cider vinegar > shred & mix: Shred the pork. Mix the reserved juices, liquid smoke, and vinegar, then toss with the pork.
shred & mix > portion: Portion the pork for freezing into {=½ cup} or {=1 cup} Souper Cubes. Refrigerate until cold, then freeze. Thaw in the fridge and reheat to {=165°F} before broiling.
portion > broil: Spread the pork you are serving in a thin layer on a rimmed metal pan. Broil until the edges brown. Serve with mustard barbecue sauce on the side. ~5 min [broiler]
