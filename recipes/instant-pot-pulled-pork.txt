Instant Pot pulled pork
portions 24
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
4.5 kg boneless skinless Boston butt, weighed after removing the bone and trimming excess fat
pantry:
520 g unsalted chicken stock
19 g Worcestershire sauce
1 g hickory liquid smoke
17 g apple cider vinegar
mustard barbecue sauce, to lightly coat the pork
spices:
36 g salt
16 g smoked paprika
14 g garlic powder
11 g onion powder
6 g ground black pepper
1 g cayenne

boneless skinless Boston butt > cut pork: Cut the trimmed pork into roughly {=5 cm} chunks.
cut pork + salt + smoked paprika + garlic powder + onion powder + ground black pepper + cayenne > season: Toss the pork with the salt and spices.
season + unsalted chicken stock + Worcestershire sauce > pressure cook: Add the stock, Worcestershire sauce, and pork to the pot. Cook on High Pressure for {=45} minutes. ~45 min [8-quart Instant Pot]
pressure cook > release: Natural release. If the pork doesn’t shred easily, cook on High Pressure for {=10} more minutes, then natural release again.
release > save juices: Remove the pork. Skim the fat from the cooking liquid and reserve about {170 g} of the juices.
save juices + hickory liquid smoke + apple cider vinegar > shred & mix: Shred the pork. Mix the reserved juices, liquid smoke, and vinegar, then toss with all the shredded pork.
shred & mix + mustard barbecue sauce > broil: Spread all the shredded pork in a loose, even layer about {=1 cm} thick on a rimmed metal pan. Broil {=one} tray at a time until the edges brown, about {=5 minutes} per tray. Toss all the browned pork with enough mustard barbecue sauce to lightly coat it. ~5 min [broiler]
broil > portion: Portion the pork you’re freezing into your {=½-cup} and {=1-cup} Souper Cubes. Refrigerate the trays until cold, then freeze until solid. For later meals, reheat the pork to {=165°F}.
