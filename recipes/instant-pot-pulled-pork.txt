Instant Pot pulled pork
portions 24
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
10 lb boneless skinless Boston butt, shopping weight before trimming
pantry:
520 g unsalted chicken stock, use {=520 g} in each batch in the {=8}-quart Instant Pot
18 g Worcestershire sauce
1 g hickory liquid smoke
16 g apple cider vinegar
spices:
33 g kosher salt
15 g smoked paprika
13 g garlic powder
11 g onion powder
6 g ground black pepper
1 g cayenne

boneless skinless Boston butt > cut pork: Trim the outside fat to at most {=6 mm}. Leave the marbling. Cut the thawed pork into {=5 cm} chunks.
cut pork + kosher salt + smoked paprika + garlic powder + onion powder + ground black pepper + cayenne > season: Toss the pork with the dry spices.
season + unsalted chicken stock + Worcestershire sauce > pressure cook: Add the stock, Worcestershire sauce, and pork to the pot. Cook on High Pressure for {=45} minutes, in batches if needed to stay below PC MAX. ~45 min [8-quart Instant Pot]
pressure cook > release: Let the pressure release naturally. If a large chunk doesn’t pull apart with a fork, cook on High Pressure for {=10} more minutes, then let the pressure release naturally again.
release + hickory liquid smoke + apple cider vinegar > shred: Shred the pork with forks. Skim the fat from the pot juices. Stir the liquid smoke and cider vinegar into {154 g} of the skimmed juices and toss with the pork. Refrigerate the pork you’re freezing in shallow containers with its juices. Once cold, pack it into meal-size freezer bags, press out the air, and freeze flat. Thaw in the fridge and reheat to {=165°F} before broiling.
shred > broil: Spread the pork you are serving in a thin layer on a rimmed metal pan. Broil until the edges brown. Serve with mustard barbecue sauce on the side. ~5 min [broiler]
