Instant Pot pulled pork
portions 24
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
4.5 kg boneless skinless Boston butt, trimmed weight
pantry:
19 g Worcestershire sauce
1 g hickory liquid smoke
30 g apple cider vinegar
mustard barbecue sauce, optional at the table
spices:
36 g salt
16 g smoked paprika
14 g garlic powder
11 g onion powder
6 g ground black pepper
1 g cayenne

boneless skinless Boston butt > cut pork: Cut the trimmed pork into roughly {=5 cm} chunks.
cut pork + salt > season: Toss the pork with the salt.
season > pressure cook: Cook the salted pork on High Pressure for {=45} minutes. ~45 min [8-quart Instant Pot]
pressure cook > release: Natural release.
release > save juices: Lift out the pork and reserve about {170 g} of the hot cooking juices.
save juices + smoked paprika + garlic powder + onion powder + ground black pepper + cayenne + Worcestershire sauce + hickory liquid smoke + apple cider vinegar > shred & mix: Whisk the smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and cayenne with a little of the reserved juices to make a paste. Stir in the Worcestershire sauce, liquid smoke, vinegar, and remaining reserved juices. Shred the pork and toss it with the entire mixture.
shred & mix > broil: Spread all the shredded pork in a loose, even layer about {=1 cm} thick on a rimmed metal pan. Broil {=one} tray at a time until the edges brown, about {=5 minutes} per tray. ~5 min [broiler]
broil + mustard barbecue sauce > portion & serve: Portion the pork you’re freezing into your {=½-cup} and {=1-cup} Souper Cubes. Refrigerate the trays until cold, then freeze until solid. For later meals, reheat the pork to {=165°F}. Serve the mustard barbecue sauce on the side.
