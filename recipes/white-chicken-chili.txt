White chicken chili
portions 6

meat:
3 boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut in thirds
produce:
1 medium yellow onion, chopped
1 jalapeño, seeded and finely chopped
2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
1 avocado, thinly sliced, for serving
1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped, for serving
pantry:
1 tbsp neutral oil
5 cups low-sodium chicken broth
9 oz canned green chiles, two 4.5 oz cans
30 oz canned white beans, two 15 oz cans, drained and rinsed
1/4 cup crushed tortilla chips, for serving
frozen:
1 1/2 cups frozen corn
dairy:
1/2 cup sour cream
1/4 cup Monterey Jack, shredded, for serving
spices:
1 tsp dried oregano
1 tsp ground cumin
kosher salt
freshly ground black pepper

neutral oil + medium yellow onion + jalapeño > soften: In a large pot over medium, cook until softened. ~8 min [large pot]
soften + garlic + dried oregano + ground cumin > fragrant: Add and cook, stirring, until fragrant. ~1 min
fragrant + boneless skinless chicken breasts + low-sodium chicken broth + canned green chiles + kosher salt + freshly ground black pepper > poach: Add, season, bring to a boil, then simmer uncovered until the chicken is cooked through. ~11 min
poach > shred: Move the chicken to a plate and shred with two forks.
shred + canned white beans > beans: Add the beans to the pot, bring to a simmer and cook, smashing a quarter of them, until slightly thickened. ~10 min
beans + frozen corn > corn: Add the corn and the shredded chicken and cook until heated through. ~1 min
corn + sour cream > finish: Off the heat, stir in the sour cream.
finish + avocado + fresh cilantro + crushed tortilla chips + Monterey Jack > serve: Ladle into bowls and top.
