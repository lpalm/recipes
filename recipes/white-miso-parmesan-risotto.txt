White miso Parmesan risotto
portions 2

pantry:
1 bag risotto rice, 175 to 200 g
7 g white miso
dairy:
25 g unsalted butter
60 g Parmigiano Reggiano, finely grated
spices:
0.5 g black pepper, freshly ground
kitchen:
750 ml water

water > boil: Bring to a boil in a medium pan. [medium pan]
boil + risotto rice > cook: Add the rice and cook over medium heat, no lid, stirring now and then, until the water is mostly absorbed. ~14 min
cook + white miso + unsalted butter > enrich: When the rice is nearly done and just slightly loose, stir in the miso and butter until dissolved, then turn off the heat.
enrich + Parmigiano Reggiano + black pepper > finish: Add the Parmesan and pepper and stir vigorously for 20 to 30 seconds until creamy and glossy. Serve at once.
