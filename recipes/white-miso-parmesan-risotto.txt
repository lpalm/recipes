White miso Parmesan risotto
portions 2
category Non-vegan Mains

pantry:
1 bag risotto rice, 175 to 200 g
7 g white miso
dairy:
25 g unsalted butter
60 g Parmigiano Reggiano, finely grated
spices:
0.5 g black pepper, freshly ground
kitchen:
750 ml water

water + risotto rice > cook: Bring the water to a boil in a medium pan, add the rice and cook over medium heat, no lid, stirring now and then, until the water is mostly absorbed. ~14 min [medium pan]
cook + white miso + unsalted butter + Parmigiano Reggiano + black pepper > finish: When the rice is nearly done and just slightly loose, stir in the miso and butter until dissolved, turn off the heat, add the Parmesan and pepper and stir vigorously for 20 to 30 seconds until creamy and glossy. Serve at once.
