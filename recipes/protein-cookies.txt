Protein cookies
portions 16
category Dessert

pantry:
500 g peanut butter
120 g micellar casein
60 g all-purpose flour
24 g sucralose, Splenda packets
30 g vanilla extract
8 g baking powder
36 g white miso paste
8 g xanthan gum
150 g dark chocolate chips
dairy:
4 large eggs
kitchen:
60 g water

peanut butter + micellar casein + all-purpose flour + sucralose + vanilla extract + baking powder + white miso paste + xanthan gum + large eggs + water + dark chocolate chips > dough: Mix everything but the chips into a dough, then fold in the chocolate chips. [mixer]
dough > bake: Scoop 60 g balls onto a lined baking sheet, flatten to 1/2 inch (they barely spread) and bake until the edges are slightly golden; cool 5 minutes on the sheet, then on a rack. ~15 min [oven 350 F]
