Protein cookies
portions 16

pantry:
500 g peanut butter
120 g micellar casein
60 g all-purpose flour
24 g sucralose, Splenda packets
30 g vanilla extract
8 g baking powder
36 g white miso paste
8 g xanthan gum
150 g dark chocolate chips
dairy:
4 large eggs
kitchen:
60 g water

peanut butter + micellar casein + all-purpose flour + sucralose + vanilla extract + baking powder + white miso paste + xanthan gum + large eggs + water > dough: Mix everything into a dough. [mixer]
dough + dark chocolate chips > fold: Fold in the chocolate chips.
fold > shape: Scoop 60 g balls onto a lined baking sheet and flatten to 1/2 inch; they barely spread. [baking sheet]
shape > bake: Bake until the edges are slightly golden. ~15 min [oven 350 F]
bake > cool: Cool 5 minutes on the sheet, then on a rack. ~5 min
