Spinach with sesame and garlic
portions 2
category Sides

produce:
1 lb fresh spinach, clean and stem and chop mature leaves
1 tbsp garlic, minced
pantry:
1 tbsp sesame seeds
2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
1 tbsp toasted sesame oil for serving
1 tbsp sugar
1 tbsp soy sauce
spices:
salt

sesame seeds > toast: Toast the seeds in a dry skillet, stirring, then set aside. [skillet]
toasted sesame oil + garlic + fresh spinach > wilt: Heat the oil over medium heat. Add the garlic and, when it sizzles, add the spinach. Stir until wilted and reduce the heat.
wilt + sugar + soy sauce + salt + toasted sesame oil for serving + toast > finish: Mix in the sugar and soy sauce, remove from the heat and season with salt. Serve at any temperature with the remaining oil and toasted seeds. Serves {2 to 4}.
