Beef noodle soup
portions 8
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
4 lb stew beef
produce:
250 g celery, diced
325 g carrots, diced
pantry:
1 tbsp olive oil
8 cubes beef bouillon
16 oz egg noodles, uncooked
spices:
2 tsp garlic powder
2 tsp onion powder
2 tsp black pepper
2 tsp dried rosemary
kitchen:
water, enough to cover well

stew beef + olive oil + garlic powder + onion powder + black pepper + dried rosemary > brown: Stir the beef with the oil and spices in the pot until evenly coated, then brown over medium-high on all sides; it needn't cook through. ~8 min [instant pot]
brown + celery + carrots > soften: Add the celery and carrots and cook, stirring, until the celery starts to soften. ~5 min
soften + beef bouillon + water > simmer: Add the bouillon cubes and water, bring to a simmer and cook. ~45 min
simmer + egg noodles > noodles: Add the noodles, lower to a gentle simmer, cover and cook until the noodles are tender and the beef is done. ~20 min
