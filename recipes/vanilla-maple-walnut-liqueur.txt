Vanilla maple walnut liqueur
portions 1
category Cocktail

bar:
1 cup vodka
1 cup brandy
pantry:
1/2 cup dark maple syrup
2 vanilla beans, split
2 cups walnuts

vodka + brandy + dark maple syrup + vanilla beans + walnuts > steep: Combine in a jar and steep, shaking now and then, until it tastes right; strain into a bottle. [jar]
