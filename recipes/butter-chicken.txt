Butter chicken
portions 4
category Non-vegan Mains

meat:
1 lb chicken, boneless and skinless, cut in 2 inch pieces
dairy:
1/4 cup plain yogurt
1 tbsp butter
3 tbsp butter for the onion
1/4 cup heavy cream
produce:
1 tbsp ginger garlic paste, equal ginger and garlic blended with a little oil
1 cup onion, diced
1 1/2 tbsp ginger garlic paste for the sauce
pantry:
1 tbsp olive oil
2 tsp sugar
2 cups diced tomatoes
1/4 cup cashews, soaked
spices:
1 tsp coriander powder
1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder
1 tsp cumin
1/2 tsp salt
2 tsp coriander powder for the sauce
2 tsp garam masala
1 tsp cumin for the sauce
1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder for the sauce
1/2 tsp salt for the sauce

plain yogurt + olive oil + ginger garlic paste + coriander powder + Kashmiri chili powder + cumin + salt + chicken > marinate: Mix the marinade well in a medium bowl, add the chicken, mix, and marinate at least 15 minutes or overnight. ~15 min [bowl]
butter + marinate > sear: Melt the butter in a medium pot over medium-high, sauté the chicken until just cooked and set aside. [pot]
butter for the onion + onion + ginger garlic paste for the sauce + sugar + coriander powder for the sauce + garam masala + cumin for the sauce + Kashmiri chili powder for the sauce + salt for the sauce > soften: In the same pot sauté the onion in the rest of the butter 5 minutes, add the ginger garlic paste for 1 minute, then the sugar and spices for 1 minute. ~7 min
soften + diced tomatoes > simmer: Add the tomatoes, with 1/4 cup water if thick, and simmer. ~5 min
simmer + cashews > blend: Off the heat, add the cashews and blend smooth. [blender]
blend + sear + heavy cream > finish: Back on the heat, stir in the chicken and cream and simmer. ~5 min
