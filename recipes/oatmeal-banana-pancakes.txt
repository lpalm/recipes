Oatmeal banana pancakes
portions 4
category Breakfast

pantry:
1 1/2 cups uncooked oatmeal
1 cup whole wheat flour
2 tsp baking soda
dairy:
2 cups buttermilk
2 egg whites
produce:
1 banana, mashed
spices:
1/4 tsp salt
kitchen:
oil for the griddle

uncooked oatmeal + buttermilk + egg whites > soak: Combine and let stand at least half an hour, or refrigerate up to 24 hours. ~30 min [bowl]
soak + whole wheat flour + baking soda + salt + banana > batter: Stir in just until the dry ingredients are moistened.
batter + oil for the griddle > cook: Cook on a hot, lightly oiled griddle. [griddle]
