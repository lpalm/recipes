Instant Pot mujadara
portions 4
category Other Vegan Mains

pantry:
1 cup brown lentils, whole
1 1/2 tbsp olive oil
1 cup brown basmati rice, rinsed and drained
produce:
3 shallots, thinly sliced, or 1 red onion
4 cloves garlic, rough chopped
1 small lemon, zested
spices:
2 tsp cumin
1 tsp coriander
1 tsp allspice
1/2 tsp cinnamon
1/2 tsp turmeric, optional
1/4 tsp ground ginger
1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
1 tsp dried mint, or parsley
kitchen:
3 cups water
hot water for soaking

brown lentils + hot water for soaking > soak: Cover the lentils with hot water and soak until needed. [bowl]
olive oil + shallots > caramelize: On sauté, cook the shallots in the oil, stirring constantly, until tender and slightly caramelized; set half aside for the topping. ~5 min [instant pot]
caramelize + garlic > fragrant: Add the garlic and sauté until fragrant. ~1 min
fragrant + cumin + coriander + allspice + cinnamon + turmeric + ground ginger + kosher salt + dried mint + small lemon + water > spice: Add the spices, salt, lemon zest and water and stir.
spice + soak + brown basmati rice > pressure: Drain the lentils and add them with the rice; stir, cover and cook on high pressure, then natural release at least 10 minutes. ~11 min
pressure > fluff: Fluff gently with a fork and serve with the reserved shallots, olive oil, tomato, avocado, yogurt or zhoug and herbs.
