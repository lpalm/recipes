Edamame with miso sesame sauce
portions 2

frozen:
1 lb frozen edamame
pantry:
1 tbsp white miso
1 tbsp soy sauce
2 tbsp rice vinegar
1 tbsp sesame oil
2 tsp maple syrup
1/2 tbsp chili oil

frozen edamame + white miso + soy sauce + rice vinegar + sesame oil + maple syrup + chili oil > cook: Put everything in a pan and cook until the edamame are hot and glazed. [pan]
